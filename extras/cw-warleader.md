*Source: Diamond Polished Spheres: Commander and Warleader Sphere*

*Practitioners of the Warleader sphere learn to organize, rally, and direct their allies in battle. From charismatic generals to canny tacticians, superior talent creates superior coordination.*

**Associated Skill (choose one):** Diplomacy, Intimidate, Profession (soldier).

When you first gain the Warleader sphere, you gain both the shout and tactic packages, the *battlecry* special attack action, and 1 (shout) or (tactic) talent.

The Warleader sphere uses its associated skill in place of the practitioner’s base attack bonus to determine its effects.

## Battlecry [SAA]

**Action:** 1 melee or ranged special attack action

**Effect:** If your weapon attack is successful, you can *engage tactics* or *shout* without spending additional actions.

---

## Shout Package

You gain the *shout* sphere ability.

### About Shouts

A ***shout*** is a burst of sound, whether as a cry of confrontation, supportive anthem, or mocking haranguing, that directly influences others.

### Shout

**Descriptor:** auditory [voice]
**Action:** standard action
**Range:** personal
**Duration:** see text
**Target / Area:** up to 10-ft. radius centered on you, +5 ft. / 2 associated skill ranks; **Area Type:** spread
**Saving Throw:** see text

**Effect:** Select one (shout) you know. All creatures in the effect’s radius are affected by the chosen (shout).

## Tactic Package

You gain the *engage tactics* sphere ability.

### About Tactics

A ***tactic*** alters how you and your allies approach an encounter, defend their positions, or press the advantage. Even when not actively coordinating your allies, you passively oversee and empower your allies.

A *tactic* is a sphere effect that is activated through *engage tactics*. During any round you use *engage tactics*, your *tactic* provides the chosen (tactic)’s ***tactical presence*** and ***tactical coordination***. When *engage tactics* ends, your *tactic* continues passively as an aura centered on yourself providing the most recently chosen (tactic)’s *tactical presence*.

Unless otherwise stated, you may only have a single active *tactic*.

### Engage Tactics

**Descriptor:** visual
**Action:** move or swift action
**Range:** personal or medium (100 + 10 ft. per associated skill rank); (passive) personal
**Duration:** 1 round (then passive, see text)
**Target / Area:** up to close range radius centered on yourself or a space within range; (passive) up to 10 ft. radius, +5 ft. / 5 associated skill ranks; **Area Type:** emanation

**Effect:** You begin your *tactic*, modifying it with a single (tactic) you possess. Your *tactic* provides the chosen (tactic)’s *tactical presence* and *tactical coordination*.

When you use a (tactic)’s augment, that augment only lasts for 1 round (even if the effect’s duration would be extended).

*Passive Tactics:* When *engage tactics* ends, your *tactic* continues passively as an aura centered on yourself, providing the *tactical presence* of your most recent (tactic). Your *tactic* is passively maintained this way until you begin a new tactic, end it as a free action, or you become helpless, paralyzed, stunned, or unconscious.

## Warleader Sphere-Specific Tags

The Polished Warleader sphere introduces a number of tags unique to this sphere.

- A **(shout)** option grants new effects to use with their *shout* sphere ability.
- A **(tactic)** option grants new effects to use with their active *tactic*, selected each time you *engage tactics*.

**Talents with Sphere-Specific Tags:** Polished Warleader sphere talents may have the (shout) or (tactic) tags listed in the talent name, indicating the talent grants an option with the relevant tag.

**Commanding Shout and Mastered Tactic:** Certain (shout) and (tactic) options may have additional effects if you possess the Commanding Shout and Mastered Tactic legendary talents. These additional effects are marked on the appropriate talent.

## Warleader Talents

### Bandmaster [utility]

**Effect:** Choose one Perform skill. You gain that Perform skill as an associated skill for the Warleader sphere.

You may use instruments appropriate to a Perform skill chosen this way with your Warleader sphere abilities. When you do, the auditory or visual components of that sphere ability are replaced with auditory [Perform skill].

The chosen Perform skills behave as follows when used as auditory components this way:

- **Act, Dance:** You use your physical movement as the instrument (this is still auditory, using the sounds and rhythms of your movements, steps, or gestures). You must have already moved at least 5 feet during your turn, and cannot be suffering from an effect that penalizes your movement (such as entangled, grappled, or immobilized, or any other effect that would reduce your speed).
- **Comedy, Oratory, Sing:** You use your voice as the instrument.
- **Keyboard, Percussion, String, Wind:** Using a physical instrument requires that you play it, occupying at least 1 hand.

**Special:** This talent may be selected multiple times, each time choosing a new Perform skill to gain as an associated skill.

### Battlecry Mastery

Whenever you perform a *battlecry*, you may *engage tactics* or *shout* before or after your attack action, and may do so even if the attack action misses.

In addition, you gain the following stance:

#### Drumbeat [stance]

**Effect:** Once per round, whenever you succeed on an attack action or attack of opportunity, you may extend the duration of one ongoing *shout* or *tactic* by 1 round.

### Decorated Commander [utility]

*The heavier metals are stained with a patina of earned blood and sweat.*

**Effect:** An army under your command gains a +2 bonus on Morale checks. When you become active with an army as its commander, you may choose one boon to grant that army (in addition to any other boons you know). You may change this boon once per week.

As the commander of an army, you may use your practitioner modifier in place of your Charisma modifier and your Warleader sphere associated skill ranks in place of your ranks in Profession (soldier) when determining the bonus to Morale checks you grant an army. When determining the number of boons you know as a commander, you may use your associated skill ranks in place of Profession (soldier).

**Associated Feat:** Inspirational Commander (*Quests and Campaigns*).

See *Ultimate Campaign*, Mass Combat and Commanders for more.

### Heraldry [utility]

**Effect:** You may wield your clan, house, nation, or other identifying symbol as your “**standard**”. Your standard must be visible and either worn (on a tabard, emblazoned on a shield or armor, and so on) or held in one hand (either holding a flagpole or a flag attached to a one or two-handed weapon) to be **presented**.

While your standard is presented, you gain a +2 circumstance bonus on Diplomacy and Intimidate checks. This bonus is increased by an additional +1 when making a Diplomacy or Intimidate check to present yourself as an important individual or as a representative of your standard's organization, +1 per 5 associated skill ranks.

In addition, you and all allies within 60 feet who can see your standard gain a +2 circumstance bonus on saving throws against charm and compulsion effects that would incite betrayal against you or your standard's organization, such as being compelled to attack allies, assist infiltrators, or reveal secrets (subject to GM discretion).

If your standard is destroyed or made unrecognizable (such as by being dirtied with a dirty trick combat maneuver or otherwise sufficiently tarnished), you do not benefit from this talent until it is restored. You may only have a single standard at a time, but may replace it with a suitable replacement while not distracted or threatened, usually requiring 1 minute of effort.

**Associated Feat:** Flagbearer (*Inner Sea World Guide*).

**Special - Banner:** The Heraldry talent is treated as the banner class feature for the purposes of other effects and prerequisites. Your standard and its 60-foot radius effect are treated as your banner and banner effect for this purpose. If you have the banner class feature, you may designate your banner as your standard.

### Press The Attack

*Keep firing until you cannot see the life in their eyes.*

**Effect:** Whenever you expend martial focus to use a Warleader sphere ability, until the end of your next turn, you can make an additional attack as a free action at your full base attack bonus with a -2 penalty.

At 10 associated skill ranks, you may now make 2 additional attacks (but only one such attack each round, such as one the turn you use this ability and the other before the end of your next turn).

### Pride Leader [stance]

*The lion stalks its prey and leads its pride to victory.*

You gain the following stance:

**Enter:** Upon entering this stance, you may allow another ally to move up to 10 feet, +5 feet per 5 associated skill ranks (to a maximum of their normal speed). This movement must be made to move closer to, threaten, or flank a creature you also threaten.

**Benefit:** While maintaining this stance, once per round as a free action, you may allow another ally to move (as described above).

### Signal Coordinator

You gain the following options:

#### Complex Commands

*Your ability to coordinate and lead is not limited to just spoken words or gestures, but instead a mix that makes it easily understood.*

This option may be added to either *engage tactics* or *shout*.

**Descriptor(s):** auditory and/or visual (minimum 1)
**Area:** (if both auditory and visual) +5 feet, +5 feet per 5 associated skill ranks

**Author’s Note and Example:** This option allows you to change a *shout*’s or *tactic*’s required components, selecting auditory, visual, or both at your discretion. Changing a sphere ability's descriptors may alter which creatures it affects. For example, a *shout* is normally only an auditory effect and deaf creatures are unaffected, but adding visual components would allow a *shout* to affect deaf creatures. Complex Commands lets you optionally add the visual descriptor (so the ability has both) or swap the auditory descriptor for the visual descriptor alone.

#### Shaped Commands

*When sound is shaped, it travels further.*

This option modifies either *engage tactics* or *shout*.

**Target / Area:** Cone-shaped area equal to 2-times the sphere ability’s normal radius or line-shaped area equal to 4-times the sphere ability’s normal radius.

**Note:** For a *shout*, the cone or line’s direction is chosen when used. For a *tactic*, the cone or line’s direction is chosen each time you *engage tactics* and remains fixed until you *engage tactics* again.

### Soldier’s Countenance [utility]

*You hold yourself with an air of dignity and respect that others immediately recognize and maybe even respect.*

You gain the following approach and option:

#### Tempered Beseechment [utility]

*A request best answered in kind.*

**Effect:** Whenever you attempt a Diplomacy check to make a request, treat the target as 1 step more friendly for the purpose of your request and its DC. You take no penalty for attempting Diplomacy with a group (*Spheres of Guile*).

You may also make requests of hostile or unfriendly creatures, but only to request deescalation of a battle or conflict, or for related agreements such as the return of allied bodies, the treatment of captives, injured, and noncombatants, and similar requests (subject to GM discretion).

**Normal:** Diplomacy checks to make a request require the target to be indifferent or friendlier.

#### Verbal Counter [approach] [utility]

*You do not tolerate lies and deceit.*

**Effect:** Whenever you successfully oppose a creature’s Bluff check that uses speech, such as lying, communicating a secret message, or making a troubling claim (*Spheres of Guile*), you may expose that creature’s lie. You may use your associated skill ranks in place of your Sense Motive (or other relevant skill) to notice or disbelieve the creature’s Bluff check.

Any creature that witnesses you expose the lie is also treated as having successfully opposed that Bluff check. For 1 day per associated skill rank, those creatures may use your associated skill bonus in place of their own Sense Motive or other relevant skill against that creature, provided they are opposing checks made to hide, lie, or deceive them.

This approach does not allow you to counter Bluff checks made during combat, such as feinting or creating a diversion, but you may still counter a diversion attempted outside of combat, such as a pickpocket calling out a distraction to escape.

### Wargames

You gain the following option and plan:

#### Combat Stratagem [plan]

**Effect:** When you reveal this plan, you both *engage tactics* and *shout*. Each of these sphere abilities become a sustained effect with a duration of 1 minute, +1 minute per 5 associated skill ranks. Instantaneous effects do not have their duration extended this way; a continuous *tactic* remains in effect even if you *engage tactics* again.

You may only reveal this plan once per encounter. If you reveal this plan again while a previous combat stratagem is ongoing, all previously created effects end.

#### Tactical Rebrief

**Effect:** Other allies benefitting from your Warleader sphere effects may spend a move or swift action to restore your martial focus.

An ally restoring your martial focus this way must have an Intelligence score of 3 or greater and a number of Hit Dice equal to at least 1/2 your total Hit Dice.

## Shout and Tactic Talents

### Aggression Doctrine

You gain the following options:

#### Fierce Shout (shout)

**Duration:** 2 rounds, +1 round per 4 associated skill ranks
**Target(s):** allies

**Effect:** Allies gain a +1 morale bonus to damage rolls, +1 per 2 associated skill ranks, on their first attack made each round.

- **Commanding Shout:** For the duration, allies gain half this (shout)’s bonus to damage rolls as a bonus to critical confirmation rolls. An ally may expend this (shout) to roll twice and take the better result on a critical confirmation roll.

#### Synchronized Strikes (tactic)

**Tactical Presence:** Allies' successful weapon attacks cause unexposed creatures to become “**exposed**” for 1 round. When another ally makes a weapon attack against an exposed creature, it is an **“exposing strike”**, which ignores 2 points of damage reduction and hardness, +1 per 5 associated skill ranks, and removes exposed.

**Tactical Coordination:** Exposing strikes now ignore all damage reduction and hardness.

- **Augment 1 mf [Ringing Strike]:** Exposed is not removed by successful attacks (expiring normally after 1 round).
- **Mastered Tactic:** If an ally reduces an exposed creature to 0 or fewer hit points, they may immediately apply the exposed condition to another creature within their threatened area.

### Alertness Doctrine (shout, tactic)

*Never unaware. Never out of position.*

You gain the following options:

#### On Alert (tactic)

**Tactical Presence:** Allies gain a +2 circumstance bonus on initiative checks and Perception checks, +1 per 10 associated skill ranks. This Perception bonus is doubled on checks to avoid being surprised (such as to find hidden enemies you are unaware of, avoid ambushes, and so on).

**Tactical Coordination:** Before taking any actions on their turn, an ally may exchange their place in the initiative order with another willing ally benefitting from this (tactic). The willing ally’s turn must be later in the initiative order. This is treated as choosing to delay, and the willing ally may then immediately take their turn.

An ally may only benefit from this effect once per round, either as the one delaying their turn or the one being given a higher initiative.

- **Mastered Tactic (requires 5 associated skill ranks):** Whenever two allies exchange their place in the initiative order, the ally whose place in the initiative order was lowered may either make a Perception check (as if taking a move action to actively search) or benefit from the total defense action until they start their turn later in the initiative order.

**Associated Feat:** Trade Initiative (*Distant Shores*).

#### Stay Alert! (shout)

**Duration:** instantaneous, plus 2 rounds, +1 round per 4 associated skill ranks (see text)
**Target(s):** allies

**Effect:** Affected allies immediately wake up, are no longer fascinated, and are no longer flat-footed if they have not acted yet. When an ally is first affected by this (shout), as an immediate action, they may either retrieve a piece of equipment or gear, make a Perception check, or stand up from prone (provoking attacks of opportunity as normal).

For the duration, allies may roll twice and take the better result on Perception checks and on saving throws against being fascinated or falling asleep (and may make such saving throws even if the effect would not ordinarily offer a saving throw). In addition, if an ally succeeds on a Perception check, all other affected allies that can hear the successful ally are treated as having also succeeded on that Perception check (this requires the successful ally to inform other allies of their successful Perception check, either by speaking or another form of communication).

- **Augment 1 mf [Group Pinpoint] (requires 10 associated skill ranks):** Allies may roll twice and take the better result on percentile rolls made against miss chance or concealment. In addition, allies automatically pinpoint the square of other creatures if two or more affected allies are within 30 feet of that creature, such as pinpointing invisible creatures, creatures obscured by total concealment, and so on.
- **Commanding Shout:** For the duration, allies are immune to magical sleep and the fascinated condition. Allies no longer need to spend an immediate action when they first benefit from this (shout) to retrieve a piece of equipment or gear, make a Perception check, or stand up from prone. An ally may expend this (shout) to immediately retrieve a piece of equipment or gear, make a Perception check, or stand up from prone (as though first affected by this shout again).

**Associated Feat:** Triangulate (*Inner Sea Races*).

### Antimagic Doctrine (shout, tactic)

*The only good mage is a dead mage. The next best thing is to not let them get a word off… it’s better they bite their tongue.*

#### Ground Them! (shout)

**Duration:** 2 rounds, +1 round per 4 associated skill ranks
**Target(s):** enemies

**Effect:** Enemies suffer a -2 penalty on all concentration and Intelligence-based skill checks for the duration, increasing by +1 per 3 associated skill ranks.

- **Augment 1 mf [Ringing Cacophany]:** This augment changes the saving throw to Will (partial); in addition to this (shout)’s normal effects, an enemy that fails their saving throw must make a concentration check whenever they cast a spell or spell-like ability or the spell is lost (DC equal to your Warleader sphere DC).
- **Commanding Shout (requires 10 associated skill ranks):** For the duration, enemies that fail a concentration check (for any reason, including this shout's effects) provoke an attack of opportunity from other creatures threatening them.

#### Warded Ranks (tactic)

**Tactical Presence:** When an ally threatens an enemy, the concentration DC to cast defensively is increased by +2, +1 per 5 associated skill ranks. This penalty increases by an additional +2 for each ally threatening that same enemy (to a maximum of twice this tactic's normal penalty).

An enemy does not suffer this penalty if the ally is unaware of the enemy's location or if the ally is unable to take an attack of opportunity against them.

**Tactical Coordination:** When an enemy begins casting a spell or using a spell-like ability, as an immediate action, allies may make a single melee or ranged attack. A successful attack deals no damage, but imposes a concentration check to not lose the spell (DC equal to your Warleader sphere DC). If multiple successful attacks are made this way, each successful attack after the first increases the DC by an additional +2 (to a maximum of twice your practitioner modifier).

- **Mastered Tactic (requires 10 associated skill ranks):** Whenever an ally benefitting from this (tactic) is targeted by an attack roll or makes a saving throw against a spell, spell-like ability, or supernatural effect, if the source of that effect is suffering penalties to their concentration checks (from any source, including this (tactic)’s *tactical presence*), that ally receives half that penalty as a bonus to their AC or saving throws (minimum 1) against that effect.

**Associated Feat:** Disruptive (*PRPG Core Rulebook*).

### Control Doctrine (shout, tactic)

*Choose the fight they're forced into, not the one they planned.*

You gain the following options:

#### Face Me! (shout)

**Descriptor:** mind-affecting
**Duration:** 2 rounds, +1 round per 4 associated skill ranks
**Target(s):** enemies
**Cost:** 1 mf
**Saving Throw:** Will (partial)

**Effect:** All enemies suffer a -1 penalty on attack rolls that do not include you as a target, -1 per 4 associated skill ranks. A successful saving throw reduces this effect’s duration to 1 round.

If a creature would be incapable of targeting you with their attacks, they ignore this penalty (such as if you are flying out of reach of a bear, but not if you were simply outside that creature’s reach and they could attempt to approach and attack you).

- **Commanding Shout (requires 5 associated skill ranks):** For each affected enemy, if that enemy attacks a creature other than you, you may expend this (shout) to force that enemy to roll twice on their attack roll, taking the worse result.

  **Special - Guardian ((challenge) package) or challenge class feature:** This (shout)’s effects are now also treated as your Guardian sphere *challenge* or challenge class feature for the purpose of other effects and abilities (but not the primary effects of that feature, such as applying order abilities, other Guardian sphere talents, and so on).

#### Tandem Setup (tactic)

**Tactical Presence:** Once per round, allies' successful attacks cause the target to become battered for 1 round.

**Tactical Coordination:** Choose one combat maneuver (except dirty trick or grapple). Whenever an ally makes a successful attack action, they may attempt the chosen combat maneuver against the struck target as a free action. This does not allow an ally to perform a combat maneuver at range if they could not already do so.

At 10 associated skill ranks, when an ally successfully performs a combat maneuver this way, the struck target provokes attacks of opportunity from other allies threatening the target.

- **Mastered Tactic (requires 10 associated skill ranks):** Allies benefitting from this (tactic) roll twice and take the better result on combat maneuvers made against battered creatures.

### Covert Doctrine (shout, tactic)

*Strike without warning.*

You gain the following options:

#### Covert Operations (tactic)

**Tactical Presence:** Allies gain a +2 circumstance bonus on Acrobatics, Escape Artist, and Stealth checks, +1 per 5 associated skill ranks. In addition, allies may use your associated skill ranks in place of their own ranks in Stealth (calculating their bonus normally).

**Tactical Coordination:** Allies may use Acrobatics to move through threatened squares at full speed without penalty and may use Stealth to move at full speed without penalty.

- **Augment 1 mf [Like Hitting Smoke]:** When an ally benefits from concealment or miss chance, that miss chance is rolled an additional time, taking the more favorable result for the ally.
- **Mastered Tactic (requires 10 associated skill ranks; Scout (Lurker, Vanish, Walk Unseen) or hide in plain sight class feature):** Allies benefitting from this (tactic) may use Stealth to hide even if they lack cover or concealment. If the ally is observed, they may still hide, but roll twice and take the worse result.

**Associated Feat:** Stealth Synergy (*Ultimate Combat*).

#### Over Here! (shout)

**Descriptor:** emotion, mind-affecting (enemies only)
**Duration:** 2 rounds, +1 round per 4 associated skill ranks
**Target(s):** you, allies, and enemies (see text)
**Saving Throw:** Will (negates) (enemies only)

**Effect:** Enemies become uncertain (*Spheres of Guile*) for the duration (Will negates). Mindless creatures and creatures immune to mind-affecting (emotion) effects are susceptible to this (shout); such creatures gain a +4 bonus on their saving throw and suffer no additional effects if successful.

For the duration, other allies may attempt Stealth checks to hide against enemies, even if they lack cover or concealment. Distracted enemies suffer a -20 penalty on their Perception check to notice or locate an ally attempting to hide this way. When an ally is first affected by this (shout), they may attempt to hide as an immediate action.

For the duration, you automatically fail any Stealth checks made to hide.

- **Augment 1 mf [Brazen Display]:** This augment changes the saving throw to Will (partial); affected enemies are shocked (*Spheres of Guile*) for 1 round and uncertain for the remaining duration (a successful Will save reduces this to only 1 round of uncertain).
- **Commanding Shout (requires 10 associated skill ranks):** When moving through an affected enemy’s threatened area, other allies benefitting from this (shout) may outwit that enemy to not provoke attacks of opportunity due to their movement. Other allies may expend this (shout) as part of making a Stealth check to hide to also gain the benefits of *invisibility* for 1 round. This is a supernatural effect.

### Deescalation Doctrine (shout, tactic)

*Not every battle is won with blades.*

You gain the following options:

#### Avoidance Strategy (tactic)

**Tactical Presence:** Allies gain a +1 circumstance bonus to their AC against attacks of opportunity, +1 per 5 associated skill ranks.

**Tactical Coordination:** If an ally would provoke an attack of opportunity, the attack of opportunity is rolled twice, taking the worse result.

- **Augment 1 mf [Missed Connections]:** When an enemy misses an attack of opportunity against an ally benefitting from this (tactic), they lose all remaining attacks of opportunity for the round (such as bonus attacks of opportunity granted by the Combat Reflexes feat). If they would have 0 remaining attacks of opportunity after missing their attack, they are instead battered.
- **Augment 1 mf [Watch Your Back]:** This (tactic)’s bonuses also apply to AC against flanking enemies (to a maximum bonus equal to the enemy’s bonus to attack rolls when flanking). If a flanking creature misses an ally with an attack, they provoke an attack of opportunity from that ally.
- **Mastered Tactic:** Whenever an ally benefitting from this (tactic) is missed by an attack of opportunity, they may immediately take a 5-foot step. This does not interrupt an ally’s movement if they were attacked while moving.

**Associated Feat:** Back to Back (*Ultimate Combat*).

**Example:** A flanking enemy receives a +3 to attack rolls when flanking. This (tactic) provides a +4 to AC against attacks of opportunity, and the watch your back augment would provide the flanked ally with a +3 to AC against the flanking enemy (not the full +4 bonus).

#### Stand Down! (shout)

**Descriptor:** mind-affecting
**Duration:** instantaneous, plus 2 rounds, +1 round per 4 associated skill ranks
**Target(s):** enemies
**Saving Throw:** Will (negates)

**Effect:** Enemies are battered for 1 round and lose their martial focus (Will negates). For the (shout)’s duration, whenever an affected enemy would regain their martial focus, they fail to regain their martial focus (Will negates).

- **Augment 1 mf [Sap Focus]:** Whenever an enemy fails their saving throw against this effect, you regain your martial focus.
- **Commanding Shout (requires 15 associated skill ranks):** For the duration, an enemy that is not maintaining martial focus (that otherwise could maintain martial focus) rolls twice and takes the worse result on any d20 check they attempt, except for saving throws made to regain their martial focus.

### Defense Doctrine (shout, tactic)

*The bulwark shall never falter.*

You gain the following options:

#### Shield Wall (tactic)

**Tactical Presence:** Allies gain a +1 shield bonus to their AC, +1 per 10 associated skill ranks. This bonus does not count as wielding a shield and stacks with other shield bonuses to AC.

**Tactical Coordination:** Allies further increase this shield bonus by +1 for each adjacent ally also benefitting from this effect (to a maximum of 2-times this (tactic)’s normal shield bonus to AC).

- **Mastered Tactic:** Contiguous adjacent allies benefitting from this (tactic) are treated as being adjacent for all beneficial purposes (for example, a line formation of 4 allies would have those allies treat each other as adjacent provided it is an unbroken contiguous chain; this would count for adjacency for effects such as the *tactical coordination*’s total AC bonus). In addition, adjacent allies benefitting from this (tactic) determine the DC to pass through their threatened area or square as equal to the highest CMD among adjacent allies.

**Associated Feat:** Swarm Scatter (*Blood of the Moon*).

**Example:** The *tactical presence* at 10 ranks grants a +2 shield bonus to AC. The *tactical coordination* would allow an ally to gain a further +4 AC from 4 adjacent allies (granting a total shield bonus to AC of +6).

#### Stand Firm! (shout)

**Duration:** instantaneous plus 2 rounds, +1 round per 4 associated skill ranks (see text)
**Target(s):** allies

**Effect:** Allies increase the DC of skill checks made to inflict conditions on them (such as Intimidate to demoralize or Bluff to feint) by 1/2 your associated skill ranks. This bonus also applies to the DC of Acrobatics checks made to move through an ally’s threatened area or square, and other similar hostile skill checks made against a target (subject to GM discretion).

In addition, choose a single **multiple-stage condition**. Allies reduce the chosen condition’s penalties, treating the condition as 1 step less severe (or ignore the penalties if it would be the condition’s first stage). Ignoring or reducing a condition’s penalties does not remove the condition. An ally is still treated as being affected by that condition for other effects (such as when entering a rage while fatigued or being shaken for Gladiator sphere (demoralization) effects).

For the purposes of this talent, a “multiple-stage condition” includes fear (shaken > frightened > panicked), fatigue (fatigued > exhausted), and if using *Spheres of Guile*, anger (angry > livid > enraged > frenzied) and disoriented (uncertain > shocked > dumbfounded). At GM discretion, other related conditions may be treated as multiple-stage conditions.

**Example:** If you choose fear, allies could ignore shaken’s penalties or treat frightened as having the penalties associated with shaken (instead of frightened’s normal penalties and effects). An affected ally may always choose to ignore this (shout), such as wanting to be angry to benefit from its circumstantial bonuses.

- **Augment 1 mf [Harangue]:** Instead of this (shout)’s normal effects, as an instantaneous effect, each ally may attempt an additional saving throw against one non-permanent, negative effect, using the effect’s original DC. A successful saving throw made this way is treated as a successful saving throw against the original effect (and may remove that effect, such as succeeding against a spell or preventing exposure to a poison). A failed saving throw made this way does not worsen the effect (such as for a disease or poison). An ally can only benefit from the harangue augment once per individual effect.
- **Commanding Shout (requires 5 associated skill ranks):** This (shout) now increases the DC of skill checks made to inflict conditions by your associated skill ranks, instead of 1/2 your associated skill ranks. When a creature fails a skill check made to inflict a condition against an ally, that ally may expend this (shout) to make a single attack at their full base attack bonus against that creature.

### Fearmonger Doctrine (shout, tactic)

*Show them why you are notorious.*

You gain the following options:

#### Daunting Presence (tactic)

**Tactical Presence:** Enemies suffering from a negative mind-affecting condition suffer a -1 penalty on attack rolls and to the saving throw DCs of their abilities against allies benefitting from this (tactic). This penalty increases by 1 per 10 associated skill ranks.

**Tactical Coordination:** Whenever an ally hits an enemy suffering from a mind-affecting condition, any number of the struck enemy’s negative mind-affecting conditions have their duration extended by 1 round (Will negates). This effect can only occur once per round per enemy.

- **Mastered Tactic (requires 5 associated skill ranks):** Instead of extending the duration of an enemy’s negative mind-affecting conditions, the ally may instead force another creature who can see or hear the struck creature to gain each of that enemy’s negative mind-affecting conditions with the same duration as the original enemy’s (Will negates).

**Associated Feat:** Demoralizing Lash (*Advanced Race Guide*).

**Note - Mind-Affecting Condition, Not “Effect”:** For the purposes of this talent, a “negative mind-affecting condition” includes fear conditions (shaken, frightened, panicked) but also disorientation conditions such as uncertain or enraged (*Spheres of Guile*). Conditions inflicted by a mind-affecting effect are not inherently a mind-affecting condition (such as paralysis or dazed from a mind-affecting source would not be a mind-affecting condition). Self-imposed conditions or effects (such as a barbarian’s rage) are not further penalized by this (tactic).

#### Fear Me! (shout)

**Descriptor(s):** fear, mind-affecting
**Duration:** 2 rounds, +1 round per 4 associated skill ranks
**Target(s):** enemies

*Note - What is Asynchronous Movement?:* Asynchronous movement may be spent at any point during a creature’s turn, provided it is not during another action, or may be spent as additional movement during a move action to move (treating the asynchronous movement as a normal bonus to a creature’s movement speed). ## Polished Warleader Interactions and Design

The Polished Warleader sphere's effects are not improved by the **Troop Commander (squadron)** feat.

Certain effects and abilities may directly improve the Polished Warleader sphere, but a blanket increase to the bonuses provided by the sphere was intentionally excluded to allow the sphere's talents to stand on their own.
