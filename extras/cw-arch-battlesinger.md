*Source: Diamond Polished Spheres: Commander and Warleader Sphere*

*Often found at the forefront of revolutions and in the ranks of freedom fighters, a battlesinger is well known for spurring forward their fellow troops against impossible odds.*

## Battle Fanfare (Ex)

At 1st level, a battlesinger gains the skald's raging song class feature, treating their battlesinger level as their skald level when determining its effects. The battlesinger's level stacks with skald levels when determining the effects of the raging song class feature, as well as determining what raging songs they know. If a raging song would require a Perform check, the battlesinger may make a Warleader sphere associated skill check instead.

At 3rd level, the battlesinger gains Battle Fanfare as a bonus feat. Allies benefitting from the battlesinger's raging song are also treated as benefitting from the battlesinger's Warleader sphere effects (such as determining when to benefit from an enhanced tactic or battlefield specialty).

At 9th level, a battlesinger can start a raging song as a move action instead of a standard action. At 15th level, a battlesinger can start a raging song as a swift action instead.

This replaces lingering commands and battlefield specialty.

## Battlesinger's Inspiration (Ex)

At 5th level, the battlesinger gains the skald's rage power class feature, but only gains a single rage power. The battlesinger uses their battlesinger level as their effective skald level when qualifying for and determining the effects of rage powers, as well as when qualifying for feats that require the rage power class feature. The battlesinger gains an additional rage power every 6 levels after 5th (11th and 17th level).

Like a normal skald, the battlesinger may use these rage powers and may share them with allies under the effects of the battlesinger's inspired rage.

This replaces commander's inspiration.

## Battlesinger Enhanced Tactics

The battlesinger has unique and exclusive access to the following enhanced tactic:

### Tactical Fervor (requires battlesinger's inspiration)

The battlesinger gains Extra Rage Power as a bonus feat.
