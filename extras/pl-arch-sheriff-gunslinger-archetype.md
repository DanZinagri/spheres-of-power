*Source: [Polished Leadership](https://www.drivethrurpg.com/en/product/585162/diamond-polished-spheres-leadership-sphere)*

*The long arm of the law is nothing without their deputies, because you can't just buy good help these days.*

## Proficiencies

A sheriff receives a martial tradition, gaining proficiency with simple weapons, light armor, bucklers, and all firearms, in addition to the combat talents granted by their martial tradition.

This modifies proficiencies.

## Combat Training (Ex)

A sheriff is considered an adept practitioner, gaining combat spheres and talents as appropriate.

This replaces the gunslinger's bonus feats.

**Special - Spheres of Guile:** If using *Spheres of Guile*, the sheriff can choose to instead gain the blended training class feature, selecting combat or skill talents. The sheriff uses their practitioner modifier as their operative modifier.

## Deputize (Ex)

At 1st level, the sheriff gains the Leadership sphere, selecting the (cohort) package. The sheriff cannot choose a drawback that trades away the ability to *recruit* a cohort. The sheriff gains an additional Leadership talent at 6th level and every 4 levels thereafter.

Beginning at 2nd level, the sheriff may now deputize their cohorts. A deputy receives proficiency with all firearms and gains the grit and deeds class features, treating their effective gunslinger level as 1/2 the sheriff's level when determining which deeds they can access and the effects of those deeds.

This replaces nimble.

## Lawman (Ex)

At 4th level, the sheriff gains a +3 circumstance bonus on Diplomacy and Intimidate checks made to deescalate violence, keep the peace, or otherwise convince others to follow the law. This bonus increases by +1 per 4 sheriff levels after 4th.

**Special:** If the sheriff is the lawful police or peacekeeper of a settlement, this bonus doubles within that settlement and a radius up to 1 mile per 2 sheriff levels (provided the area is still beholden to that settlement's laws). The same applies if the sheriff is a lawful police or peacekeeper of a larger populace or country (subject to GM discretion).

## Reliable Backup (Ex)

At 7th level, whenever the sheriff or one of their deputies regains a point of grit, each other deputy and the sheriff also regains a point of grit.

This replaces the targeting deed.
