#### Drill Sergeant

**Prerequisite:** (cohort) package.

You cannot *recruit* cohorts and can only be the leader of conscripted allies (using the temporary conscription option). You gain Temporary Conscription and 1 other (cohort) talent of your choice.

**Incompatible:** Strange Bedfellows.

#### Established Footprint

Your camp is a more elaborate guild hall, office, or other establishment that cannot be moved so simply.

**Prerequisite:** (follower) package.

You must *make camp* in a settlement, and it takes 1 week to *make camp*. You gain a (follower) talent as a bonus talent.

#### Strange Bedfellows

**Prerequisite:** (cohort) package.

Choose one exotic cohort type offered by the Exotic Cohort legendary talent. You gain Exotic Cohort as a bonus talent and can only *recruit* cohorts with the chosen exotic cohort type.

**Note:** This drawback offers the benefits of a legendary talent and should only be available subject to GM discretion.

**Incompatible:** Drill Sergeant.

#### Wicked Tyrant [alternate start]

Instead of selecting an associated skill, you gain Intimidate as your associated skill for this sphere. With this alternate start, you lead your cohorts and followers using fear, and as such, cannot maintain contacts. Subject to GM discretion, the allies, family, and friends of *recruited* cohorts may also withhold services or refuse to interact with you (especially if the cohort was mistreated or killed in your service).

If using the optional rule **Associated Skill Checks to *Recruit* Cohorts**, you do not suffer any penalties to *recruit* a cohort due to mistreatment or cohort death.
