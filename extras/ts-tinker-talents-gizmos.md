#### Alcohol Set (gizmo, synergy) [utility] [DRS]

*Source: [Diamond Spheres: Expanded Tinker and Silverminds](https://www.drivethrurpg.com/en/product/556860/diamond-spheres-expanded-tinker-and-silverminds)*

*The best inventions were made with a buzz, or give you that buzz.*

**Recommended Sphere:** Barroom sphere.

**Biofuel:** Batteries you craft can be drawn and drunk as an alcoholic drink, depleting them.

**Distillery (gizmo, project):** You may craft a distillery capable of creating strong, potent alcohol. A distillery requires 50 gp in project materials. Each distillery can convert 1 drink of water into 1 drink of alcohol once per day in an hour-long process.

- **Battery Use (2 batteries):** The distillery produces 1 drink of alcohol in 1 minute. This does not count against the distillery’s daily limit of alcohol produced.

A distillery can instead be crafted as a more complex model, requiring 250 gp in project materials per gizmo level. When crafted this way, the distillery produces high-grade alcohol of a certain variety (chosen when crafted).

- **Feelsgood Brew:** The imbiber gains a +2 alchemical bonus on Fortitude and Will saving throws for 1 round per gizmo level. This bonus ends after the imbiber makes a Fortitude or Will saving throw.
- **Healthy Fermentation:** This alcohol also functions as an antiplague and antitoxin. The alcohol’s benefits (as alchemical remedies) last for 1 hour per 2 gizmo levels (minimum 1 hour).
- **Straight Moonshine:** The drunk status gained with this alcohol can be expended an additional time before losing the drunk status, +1 additional time per 10 gizmo levels. This alcohol is also treated as having that many drinks.
- **Trendy Spirit:** This alcohol grants the imbiber a +2 alchemical bonus on Bluff, Diplomacy, and Intimidate checks, increasing by +1 per 5 gizmo levels. These benefits last for 1 hour, +1 hour per 4 gizmo levels.

**Augmentation: Drinking Aid:** This option modifies a physical augmentor assigned to Constitution.

The user adds twice the physical augmentor’s bonus to the number of drinks they can consume before becoming sick. If the user possesses the Barroom sphere, the user also adds twice the physical augmentor’s bonus to the duration of the drunk status.

- **Battery Use [Intense Purge] (1 battery):** The user may activate this ability as a free action. The user forcibly purges their body. The user is treated as having had no alcoholic drinks during the day (resetting the total number of drinks they’ve consumed today).

  The user may use this ability as an immediate action to also attempt a new saving throw against any number of ongoing effects caused by a failed Fortitude saving throw (effects that can worsen on a failed saving throw do not count this saving throw as a failure).

  The user cannot benefit from this battery ability more than once a minute, and cannot attempt an additional saving throw against an effect more than once per day.

**Modification: Partition Container (gizmo, minor):** This *modification* is attached to a container for liquids. The container is able to hold an equal number of 2 different liquids, +1 liquid per 10 gizmo levels. When dispensing or drinking from the container, the user may choose which liquid is dispensed.

**Transmission:** You learn to craft the following *detector*:

- **Alcohol Sensor (gizmo):** This *detector* detects alcohol. Determine the detected alcohol’s signature strength using **Table: Mechanical Signature Strength**. In addition to learning the signature strength, the user may attempt a Profession (brewer) check to identify the type of alcohol and its properties.

**Transportation:** You learn to craft *mechanoids* with the following upgrade:

- **Drink Bus (upgrade):** The *mechanoid* is crafted with 2 distilleries, plus up to 1 additional distillery per 4 gizmo levels. Each distillery’s project materials must still be supplied. The *mechanoid* may dispense drinks produced with a distillery gizmo into an adjacent square (or directly into an appropriate vessel). An ally within the *mechanoid’s* natural reach can retrieve and drink an alcoholic drink, drinkable alchemical, elixir, or potion (but not extract) stored on the *mechanoid* as a move action. Retrieving and drinking a substance this way does not provoke attacks of opportunity.

**Special - Excavation Set (Internal Pump):** If the *mechanoid* possesses both this upgrade and the Excavation Set’s internal pump upgrade, the *mechanoid* may dispense alcohol directly from its internal storage containers as though it were an ordinary liquid.

#### Animal Set (gizmo, synergy) [utility] [DRS]

*Source: [Diamond Spheres: Expanded Tinker and Silverminds](https://www.drivethrurpg.com/en/product/556860/diamond-spheres-expanded-tinker-and-silverminds)*

**Recommended Sphere:** Beastmastery sphere.

**Animal Supply (requires Arsenal Set):** You may craft arsenal gizmos that function as bits and bridles, saddles (including specialized saddles, such as exotic or military saddles), and similar basic equipment for handling or riding animals. You do not need a previous example to produce these items as arsenal gizmos (subject to GM discretion, equipment that is not appropriate for the setting may still be restricted). This also extends to animal appropriate equipment (such as barding), allowing you to produce barding provided you have an understanding of the armor type it is based on (without requiring a previous barding sample).

**Gizmo Trainer:** You may teach the Use Gizmo task to animals (or other creatures that can learn tricks, such as AI) without it counting against the subject’s total tricks known, and may teach them to use 1 gizmo, +1 gizmo per 10 ranks in the associated skill. A creature can only benefit from a single instance of this ability at one time.

**Special - Tinker Savvy:** Training or handling another creature to use gizmos is considered a technological skill use, allowing you to make a Tinker sphere associated skill check in place of Handle Animal checks as appropriate.

**Saddle Assistant (gizmo, minor):** The user may mount creatures of any size as swift action without a check. When using fast mount, they may mount creatures of any size they could ride, and may do so as long as they have their move or swift action available. If they fail, they may mount as a move or swift action.

- **Advanced Gizmo:** The user does not take a penalty for riding bareback and is treated as having the necessary basic equipment to ride and control their mount (such as a bit and bridle for a horse, or other equipment as needed).

**Portable Hostel (gizmo) [Advanced Gizmo]:** The user may use this gizmo to store a willing animal and its equipment. If the animal is unintelligent, it must be trained or pushed to be stored with the Use Gizmo trick. A portable hostel can only store a single creature at a time, and cannot store a creature carrying more than a light load.

While stored, the stored creature is treated as being in a habitable extradimensional space. It cannot take any actions except to rest or to exit the portable hostel. A user may use this gizmo again to release the stored creature into an adjacent square.

A stored creature ages and must eat and drink even while stored. A stored creature may choose to release itself into a random square adjacent to the gizmo, and is immediately released if the gizmo is destroyed or if the gizmo is not maintained for 24 hours.

**Special - Sensory Set:** The stored creature may perceive its surroundings using any sensory gizmos the portable hostel is connected to.

**Special - (Transmission) Package:** If the portable hostel has the communicator accommodation, it may use the communicator to send messages from inside the portable hostel, and messages can be sent to the portable hostel for the stored creature to receive.

**Special - Teleportation Set:** A teleporter gizmo may teleport a willing target directly into a portable hostel within signal range (provided they can be stored normally). A stored creature can be teleported when being released (instead of appearing adjacent) by depleting 1 battery as a **battery use ability**.

**Note - Storing Other Creatures:** A portable hostel may be used to store other subordinates (including tamed vermin or magical beasts, undead, familiars, and so on), but cannot store humanoid or similar allies. A portable hostel can never store an unwilling creature.

**Augmentation: Trick Imprinter (gizmo, minor):** When used by a creature capable of learning tricks or tasks, the user gains knowledge of 1 trick or task, +1 per 10 gizmo levels, chosen when the gizmo is crafted. A creature can benefit from only a single trick imprinter at a time, and cannot benefit from a different trick imprinter until at least 1 minute has passed. Adjusting the selected tricks, such as with the Adjust Parameters feat, causes a trick imprinter to count as a different trick imprinter.

**Augmentation: Uplifter (gizmo) [Advanced Gizmo]:** If the user’s Intelligence is lower than 6 (including -), it is increased to 6. If the target is normally immune to mind-affecting effects due to not possessing a mind, the user loses that immunity and instead gains a +4 racial bonus against mind-affecting effects. Gaining an Intelligence score with an uplifter does not grant feats or skill points. The user also gains a single language known by the crafter, chosen when crafted. If the user’s form would allow them to speak, they may speak this language normally.

A creature under another’s control is still under that creature’s control despite being intelligent.

**Augmentation: Voicebox (gizmo):** This *augmentation* allows the user to speak and supply verbal spell components normally, even if in a form that would normally prevent this.

**Special - Sensory Set:** As an (accommodation), *augmentations* you craft may have a voicebox as an innate gizmo.

**Modification: Secure Saddler (gizmo, minor):** This *modification* is attached to a saddle or other seat used to ride another creature. When the rider would be knocked unconscious, there is a 25% chance they remain mounted, +25% per 4 gizmo levels.

- **Advanced Gizmo:** The rider gains a +2 circumstance bonus, +1 per 4 gizmo levels, on saving throws and skill checks to avoid being dismounted, and also to their combat maneuver defense against effects that would forcibly dismount them or otherwise remove them from the saddle (such as being teleported, subject to a bull rush, and so on).

**Transportation:** You learn to craft *mechanoids* with the following upgrades:

- **Hosteling (upgrade):** The *mechanoid* gains an innate portable hostel gizmo, +1 additional innate portable hostel gizmo per 10 gizmo levels.
- **Mechanized Mount (upgrade):** The *mechanoid* can be ridden as though it were a suitable mount. It must still be piloted or have an installed AI to take any actions. The *mechanoid* gains an innate advanced saddle assistant gizmo and an innate advanced secure saddler gizmo. It can be piloted with Ride or Handle Animal checks in addition to proficiency or Profession (pilot) checks.

#### Emotion Set (gizmo, synergy) [DRS]

*Source: [Diamond Spheres: Expanded Tinker and Silverminds](https://www.drivethrurpg.com/en/product/556860/diamond-spheres-expanded-tinker-and-silverminds)*

*The mind is a curious and emotional thing.*

**Recommended Sphere:** Bluster or Communication.

**Mental Disruption (requires Disruption Set):** You may choose from the following disruptor effects:

- **Enraging:** The target increases their current anger condition by 1 step (none, angry, livid, enraged, frenzied) against the user for 1 round per 2 gizmo levels (Will negates). Multiple uses of this disruptor effect can increase the target’s current anger; the morale bonus to Will saving throws does not apply to a saving throw against this disruptor effect.
  - **Battery Use (1 battery):** The target instead becomes two steps more angry against the user (Will partial). On a successful save, they increase their current anger by 1 step for only 1 round.

If the user could designate a different focus for the target’s anger, the target does not receive a bonus to their saving throw if the focus would be an ally.

**Special - (Transmission) Package; Cognitive Set or Sensory Set:** If this disruptor is a *transmitter*, when you inflict an anger condition with this gizmo, you may have the focus of the target’s anger be another creature within close range of the target (instead of the user, the source of the condition). The target gets +4 on their saving throw if the designated focus would be an ally or otherwise friendly subject. The focus of the target’s anger cannot be a creature they are unaware of. If multiple targets would be angered, the chosen focus of their anger must be the same.

- **Fascinating:** The target becomes fascinated for 1 round per 2 gizmo levels (Will negates). This disruptor effect does not work if used as part of a damaging effect (such as with a blaster or disruptor add-on). A target actively in combat receives a +4 bonus against this effect. Using this disruptor effect is not treated as a hostile action or obvious threat for the purposes of the fascinated condition.
  - **Battery Use (1 battery):** The target instead becomes fascinated for 1 round per gizmo level (Will negates). The target does not immediately break from this condition if they see an obvious threat, but instead receives a new saving throw against this effect. If the target is damaged while fascinated, the target immediately breaks free from this effect.
- **Disorienting:** The target increases their current disoriented condition by 1 step (none, uncertain, shocked, dumbfounded) for 1 round per 2 gizmo levels (Will negates). Multiple uses of this disruptor effect can increase the target’s current disorientation.
  - **Battery Use (1 battery):** The target instead becomes two steps more disoriented (Will negates). On a successful save, they instead increase their current disorientation by 1 step for 1 round.
  - **Battery Use (1 battery):** If the target fails the initial saving throw, they must make an additional saving throw or the duration becomes the normal duration of whatever disorientation condition they have.

**Augmentation & Computation: Emotional Fortifier (gizmo, routine):** The user gains a +1 insight bonus, +1 per 5 gizmo levels, on all saving throws against emotion effects and to the DC of any skill checks to inflict emotion conditions on them (such as Intimidate to demoralize).

- **Battery Use [Emotional Clarity] (1 battery):** As an immediate action, the user may attempt a new saving throw against an ongoing emotion effect. If the effect did not offer a saving throw, the saving throw DC is set by the source’s sphere DC or 10 + 1/2 the ranks in the appropriate skill + the source’s ability score modifier for that skill (or an appropriate DC subject to GM discretion). A successful save ends the emotion effect. The user may only attempt a new saving throw this way against a given emotion effect once (but may attempt it again for new instances of that effect).

  Alternatively, the user may instead choose to suppress the emotion effect for 1 round, +1 round per 4 gizmo levels. While suppressed, the emotion effect’s duration does not expire.

**Computation: Artificial Positivity (gizmo, minor, routine):** This *routine* allows the user to benefit from morale bonuses and mind-affecting effects, even if they are mindless or would otherwise be immune to mind-affecting effects. This applies equally to beneficial and harmful effects (the user cannot selectively choose to not benefit from this *routine*). This *routine* can be used without requiring any kind of training or instruction, even by mindless creatures.

**Computation: Psychosocial Advisor (gizmo, routine):** The user gets a +2 insight bonus, +1 per 4 gizmo levels, on all skill checks made to apply a condition to another creature (such as Intimidate to apply shaken, or other skill uses that inflict angry, disoriented, fascinated, impressed, and so on).

This *routine* also benefits other mind-affecting or language-dependent abilities, gizmos, or sphere abilities (such as Bluster’s *quip*, certain Communication or Study sphere uses, Gladiator *boasts*, Warleader *shouts*, and so on). The user gains this bonus on any required skill check for the ability and also gains a +1 insight bonus to the DC of the ability, +1 per 10 gizmo levels.

Any ability, gizmo, sphere ability, or skill use that benefits from this *routine* becomes a mind-affecting emotion effect (if it was not already).

- **Battery Use (1 battery):** The user may use this ability as a move action to improve this gizmo’s function for 1 round per gizmo level. For the gizmo’s duration, any emotion effects the user inflicts has their duration increased by 50% (minimum 1 round). This does not stack with other effects that increase effect duration.

**Transmission: Social Network:** Your communicators allow verbal and sound-based abilities to be broadcast through your communicators. This allows a user to treat other communicators within signal range as being within range of their abilities. If an ability would normally affect an area, it instead provides benefits to up to 2 creatures, +1 creature per 2 gizmo levels (up to the normal maximum that ability could affect). The user must spend the minimum action required for the communicator as part of using the other relevant ability (a swift or immediate action, or faster if your communicator *accommodation* could be used faster). Magical and supernatural abilities cannot be transmitted through a communicator this way (such as a bard’s bardic performance).

**Example(s):** The following abilities could be used through a communicator with this option. For the purposes of this option, a “socially networked” creature is another creature with a communicator you crafted that can receive the user’s communicator’s transmissions.

- **Bluster sphere:** The user may make *quips* to socially networked creatures.
- **Communication sphere:** The user may *build rapport* with socially networked creatures (including multiple, if multiple creatures could receive your communication).
- **Gladiator sphere:** When using a *boast*, the user instead affects themselves and other socially networked creatures (up to the maximum number a *boast* could benefit).
- **Performance sphere:** The user could use *instrumentals* or *lyrics* on socially networked creatures. *Acts* and *dances* are normally not auditory effects.
- **Warleader sphere:** When using a *shout*, the user may instead affect themselves and other socially networked creatures.

Other non-magical auditory or language dependent effects could be transmitted to socially networked creatures at GM’s discretion, such as a cavalier’s tactician ability, verbal aid another, and so on. The list above is not exhaustive.

#### Scholarly Set (gizmo, computation, synergy) [utility] [DRS]

*Source: [Diamond Spheres: Expanded Tinker and Silverminds](https://www.drivethrurpg.com/en/product/556860/diamond-spheres-expanded-tinker-and-silverminds)*

*Gone are the days of poring over a dusty tome when scholarly aids are available.*

**Recommended Sphere:** Study.

**Computation: AI Search (gizmo, routine):** This *routine* references information stored on gizmos it can access to answer questions relating to that data.

When the user would spend at least a standard action to attempt a skill check to review stored data for a specific piece of information, the user may also utilize the installed AI search to attempt to find and summarize the same information. Sought-after information must be objective (such as “find the cure to this disease”, “locate the purple hooded cultist in this video of a crowd”, “tell me how many books are scanned”, and so on). The AI search reviews and attempts appropriate skill checks based on the information searched (such as Perception to notice details, Craft (alchemy) to understand a formula is what they’re looking for, and so on). The AI search produces results within 1 round as though it made an appropriate skill check whose result is equal to 10 + 1/2 this *routine’s* gizmo level + this *routine’s* practitioner modifier. When searching, the AI search is treated as though it understands all languages the user understands (or has access to, such as through a translator *routine*). The user may continue their own check, as normal, but taking the normal time to review the information.

When an AI search produces its results, the results are presented from the available reviewed data (such as text snippets, section references, video timestamps or stills, infographics, or image collages). This result may be separately stored in a storage *routine*. If the answer could be read or otherwise understood as a full-round action or less, the user may do so as part of using this gizmo. Answers that consist of large amounts of data may require additional time for the user to read or view, at the GM’s discretion.

When producing its results, the AI search attempts to “answer” to the best of its ability, but is only able to summarize information available to it based on the sought-after information. At the GM’s discretion, if the searched information is bad, corrupted, or incorrect, the AI search’s results may similarly produce bad or incorrect information.

- **Advanced Gizmo:** An advanced AI search can use the user’s corresponding skill check modifiers when reviewing information, if better.

**Special - Cognitive Set:** The AI search is treated as possessing any number of languages from a translator *routine* installed in the AI search’s host gizmo (or within the host gizmo’s signal range, if you possess the transmission package).

**Special - (Transmission) Package:** If the host gizmo is a *transmitter*, the AI search may search information in storage *routines* (or similar data storages) within signal range.

**Computation: Data Optimization:** Your storage *routines* (and other gizmos that can store information) store 10-times more information. This does not apply to gizmos with non-specific storage capacities, such as text scanner gizmos (which can store a single written work, regardless of size).

Information stored in your gizmos (such as within a text scanner gizmo or a storage *routine*) can be read, reviewed, or otherwise interacted with 10-times faster. This does not allow the user to perform specific actions with set times more quickly (such as preparing spells, engaging with artworks, or retraining with a training manual).

When referencing a source of information in this way and for 1 hour afterwards, the user gains a +2 circumstance bonus on Knowledge checks related to the content of the information, +1 per 4 gizmo levels. If the information would already provide a nonmagical bonus based on the contained information, the user benefits from the higher bonus.

**Computation: Text Scanner (gizmo, minor):** A text scanner can be used to record written information regardless of language (by default, this does not translate, but transcribes accurately based on the text, handwriting, and so on). The scanner can record a single writing, regardless of size, in 1 minute provided that all of the information is within range (by default, within the user’s reach). The scanner can store a single source of written information, regardless of size, or can transfer its information into a storage *routine*. A scanner can also allow a user to read a magical scroll (similar to using *read magic*), but does not otherwise allow a user to cast or copy that scroll if they could not already do so.

Using a scanner is not treated as reading the text for effects that would occur in response to being read (such as magical glyph traps). If writing would be protected (magically, technologically, or otherwise), the user may attempt a gizmo penetration check against the protecting effect to successfully transcribe the writing. A failure means the scanner cannot digitally store the writing. Messages hidden by mundane means or information lost to damage or decay are revealed if they could be discovered or reconstructed with a suitable check of 15 + gizmo level + your practitioner modifier (this may reveal partial information or even merely that there once was writing on the object, at the GM’s discretion; if the information would be irretrievable, the scanner cannot record it).

- **Battery Use (1 battery):** The scanner reads a written work in 1 round.
- **Battery Use (1 battery):** The user scans 1 additional target, +1 per 3 gizmo levels. All targets must be within range (within the user’s reach, or signal range if crafted with access to the transmission package).

**Special - (Transmission) Package:** A text scanner can record writing within your *detector* range.

**Special - Cognitive Set:** An installed translator *routine* functions simultaneously with a scanner, gaining fluency from recorded writing. If the translator possesses the writing’s language, the translator can translate the writing simultaneously to the scanner recording the writing.

**Transmission:** You learn how to craft the following *detector*:

- **Writing Detector (gizmo):** This *detector* senses written information (including digitally stored written information. Determine the detected writing’s signature strength using **Table: Writing Signature Strength**. The user may pinpoint detected targets (but only for the purposes of targeting that source of written information, such as to then record with a text scanner).

##### Table: Writing Signature Strength

| Quantity of Text | Approximate Number of Words | Strength |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A few words or sentences | ~25 words | Diluted |
| A few pages of information | ~250 words | Weak |
| A typical book or a scroll or small spellbook1, such as a novice’s spellbook | ~2,500 words | Considerate |
| A massive book or a medium spellbook | ~25,000 words | Potent |
| Magical books of great size, such as an archmage’s spellbook, storage *routines* holding many books | ~250,000 words | Astounding |

1: For the purposes of approximate number of words, magical writing is 10-times an equivalent amount of mundane writing (for example, 250 words in a magical source of writing would be a “Considerate” signature strength).

#### Tool Set (gizmo, synergy) [DRS]

*Source: [Diamond Spheres: Expanded Tinker and Silverminds](https://www.drivethrurpg.com/en/product/556860/diamond-spheres-expanded-tinker-and-silverminds)*

*When you need to get a job done.*

**Recommended Sphere:** Artifice, Survivalism, or Trap.

**Multitool (gizmo):** When you craft this gizmo, choose 3 kits or tools (including tools such as a trap bag), +1 per 4 gizmo levels. This gizmo functions as a masterwork version of the chosen tools or kits. Unlike a normal masterwork tool, a tool created this way applies to all uses of the skill (nor limited to a specific purpose) In the case of kits with limited uses (such as healer’s kits or disguise kits), a multitool has 5 uses, +1 use per 2 gizmo levels, which are shared between all chosen kit types and restored whenever the multitool is maintained.

A multitool cannot function as a masterwork tool for Knowledge skills.

**Special - Modified Tool:** A modified tool gizmo attached to a multitool improves each tool or kit included in the multitool.

**Special - (Computation) Package:** Your multitools can now function as masterwork tools for Knowledge skills.

**Power Tool (gizmo):** A power tool gizmo represents a variety of powered tools meant to make handiwork faster and easier. Using any power tool creates noise (similar in volume to the sounds of battle) unless its noise is dampened or silenced, as appropriate. When a power tool is crafted, choose one of the following functions:

- **Power Cutter:** A power cutter can be used as a full-round action to remove material from a larger object (affecting up to a 5-foot area of material within your natural reach). The object must have a hardness less than or equal to 5 + 1 per gizmo level. The power cutter is treated as dealing 1 damage per gizmo level, bypassing the object’s hardness (with additional rounds being used to continue to remove the same area of material). Damage dealt to larger objects is dealt per inch of thickness, with additional damage being “applied” to the next inch (for example, attempting to cut through a 2-inch stone object with 10 hit points per inch, would require dealing 20 damage to cleanly cut through that section of the object. If the object were more than a single 5-foot square, multiple rounds would be required to fully remove the material).

  A power cutter is most frequently used to remove simple shapes (create holes, cut off corners, destroy chains or rope). Subject to GM discretion, removing intricate shapes from an object may require a Craft check (appropriate to the object) or additional time, and brittle objects may require additional time to avoid shattering the object (such as glass).

  - **Battery Use [Efficient Removal] (1 battery):** The user improves the power cutter’s functions for 1 round per gizmo level. The user may remove material once each round as a standard action.
- **Power Joiner:** A power joiner can be used as a full-round action to join two solid objects to each other or to disassemble an object into one or more parts. A power joiner may only be used on unattended objects (or objects attended by a willing or helping creature).
  - **Join:** The two objects are joined with a “joining agent” (such as with a bolt, nail, solder, and so on). The user chooses how the object is joined to create a now-singular object (such as building a bridge plank by plank, attaching a door to hinges, attaching a covering to a doorway to seal it, and so on). Once joined, the objects can be detached by dealing 3 damage per gizmo level to the joining agent or with a successful Strength check (DC equal to gizmo DC).

    Joined objects may support weight or be capable of certain mechanical functions subject to GM discretion.
  - **Disassemble:** The power joiner applies an amount of force or torque to disassemble a constructed object into one or more parts. The object must have been built using some method of joining agent (see above), and the object is disassembled based on that agent (hinges are removed, nails are removed, and so on). Using a power joiner this way is treated as attempting a Disable Device check, Knowledge (engineering) check, or Strength check as appropriate, equal to 10 + this gizmo’s gizmo level + this gizmo’s practitioner modifier to disassemble the object appropriately.

    Using the power joiner this way does not destroy the components and instead breaks them back down, subject to GM discretion. A power joiner may also be used on other constructed objects (removing a gem from a statue’s eye, unattaching a steel gate from a sewer entrance, and so on) subject to GM discretion.
  - **Battery Use [Quick Join] (1 battery):** The user may use the power joiner as a move or swift action.
- **Power Kit:** A power kit grants no benefits unless one of its battery use abilities is activated.
  - **Battery Use [Expansive Work] (1 battery):** The user may use this ability as part of using a non-magical sphere effect that would be placed in or applied to two or more 5-foot squares (such as Survivalism’s *dredge*, creating a trap with Trap sphere, and so on). The user increases the number of squares they can affect by 50%, rounded down. All squares must be within range and be suitable selections for the sphere effect (for example, a Survivalism’s *dredge* must still be contiguous squares within range).
  - **Battery Use [Larger Work] (1+ batteries):** The user may use this ability as part of using a sphere effect that crafts an item with a size-related check (such as an Artifice sphere trinket). If they do, the user treats their size as +1 larger when determining the craft time and crafting DC related to size. For every 10 gizmo levels, the user may deplete 1 additional battery to be treated as +1 additional size larger.
- **Power Sander:** A power sander can be used as a full-round action to either “roughen” or “smooth” an unoccupied 5-foot square within their natural reach (or only occupied by the user). This provokes an attack of opportunity. A power sander can be used on a reasonably flat surface (most floors, walls, and so on) provided that the surface is not cluttered (i.e. full of objects, plantlife, or other objects, such as a square of dirt filled with roots and plants).

  A surface modified with a power sander is permanently altered until fixed (similar to repairing the surface, or spending at least 1 minute with appropriate tools to correct the changes). Roughening a smoothed surface (or vice versa) removes the changes and returns the square back to normal, provided that the power sander’s gizmo level is equal to or greater than the original roughening or smoothing.

  - **Roughen:** The square’s climb DC is reduced by the gizmo’s practitioner modifier + 2, +1 per 4 gizmo levels. In addition, a roughened square grants a circumstance bonus to a creature’s saving throws and CMD against effects and maneuvers which would force them out of that square (such as bull rush or drag combat maneuvers, spell effects, etc.) by +2, +1 per 4 gizmo levels.
  - **Smooth:** The square’s climb DC is increased by the gizmo’s practitioner modifier + 2, +1 per 4 gizmo levels. When not being climbed (i.e. traversed with *spider climb* or a similar effect), a smoothed square becomes slippery, requiring an Acrobatics check (DC equal to gizmo DC) during any movement greater than half the creature’s movement. A failed check means the creature stops moving and falls prone.

**Technological Enhancement:** When you craft a non-gizmo temporary item using a combat or skill sphere, you may make that item “technologically enhanced”. A technologically enhanced item gains the functionality of a minor gizmo with no additional abilities other than those you could grant to any gizmo, such as *accommodations* or the ability to install *routines* (excluding options with special costs to apply or that require crafting the gizmo as an advanced gizmo).

Each technologically enhanced item counts against your gizmo limit as a minor gizmo but uses the statistics of the base item, not a gizmo’s normal statistics (hardness, hit points, etc.). Technologically enhanced items cannot be scavenged or used for abilities that require depleting gizmos, and become abandoned when the item’s duration expires. You may activate the gizmo as part of crafting the item.

If a technologically enhanced item consists of multiple pieces, such as a group of ammunition, only a single piece may benefit from this ability (chosen when crafted).
