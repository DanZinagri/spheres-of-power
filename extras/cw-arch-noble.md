*Source: Diamond Polished Spheres: Commander and Warleader Sphere*

*Some are born to lead. The nobility focus their direction, resources, and talents to coordinate their most loyal subordinates.*

## Lordly Station (Ex)

At 1st level, the noble gains the Warleader sphere as a bonus sphere, plus an additional Leadership or Warleader sphere talent at each odd level after 1st.

This alters battlefield operator.

## Loyal Retainers (Ex)

At 1st level, the noble gains the Leadership sphere, selecting the (cohort) package. The noble cannot choose any sphere-specific drawback that trades the ability to *recruit* a cohort, such as the drill sergeant drawback.

## Lingering Commands (Ex)

At 4th level, the noble gains lingering commands for 1 round, +1 additional round at 10th and 16th level.

This alters lingering commands.

## Noble's Oversight (Ex)

At 7th level, while within medium range (100 feet + 10 feet per noble level) of the noble, the noble's cohorts are treated as benefitting from the noble's Warleader sphere effects for the purposes of other noble class features (such as enhanced tactics or battlefield specialties).

At 19th level, this improves to long range (400 feet + 40 feet per noble level).

At 20th level, this range becomes unlimited.

## Noble's Resources (Ex)

At 13th level, the noble gains Logistics Coordination as a bonus talent. In addition, the increased enhancement bonus provided by the quartermaster's skill option is increased by an additional +1.
