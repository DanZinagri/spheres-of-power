**Diamond Spheres: Magical Organizations [DRS]:** The abilities marked [DRS] below use these rules.

This section contains new implement special abilities with a focus on the "grasped" keyword ability. Implements with these special abilities may enhance a user's magic, as they normally would, but are also conduits of their own minor tricks and abilities, a favorite of hedge wizards which rely on wands, foci, and other implements to complement their inherent talent.

**Implement Prerequisite Sphere:** Certain implement special abilities may only be placed onto an implement that meets certain prerequisites, such as being associated with a certain sphere or possessing a different special ability. Implement requirements are listed as "Prerequisite Sphere: X" in their Description.

**Implement Special Ability Keyword - Grasped:** An implement special ability with a "grasped" effect has additional effects or functions while held in hand as the user would a weapon. This means the implement must be wielded in a hand that is capable of wielding weapons and making attacks with them. Implements that have been crafted to be worn or occupy an item slot may be temporarily grasped with the user's hand, provided the user has a free hand to grasp the implement (such as a neck slot implement being held at the chest, not simply passively being worn).

For example, the additional grasped effects would not be usable when held in secondary arms from the Alteration sphere Additional Limbs talent.
