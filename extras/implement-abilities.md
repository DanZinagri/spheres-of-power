#### Bone-Tolling [DRS]

*Source: Diamond Spheres: Magical Organizations*

**Aura** moderate Death; **CL** 7th
**Price** +6,000 gp

**Description**
The implement is capable of producing sickle-green spikes of energy that radiate death.

**Prerequisite Sphere:** Death sphere.

The wielder may use a *bone-tolling* implement to make a ranged touch attack against a target within 60 feet, launching a sickly green spike of necromantic energy. This attack deals 1d6 negative energy damage per enhancement bonus and afflicts the target with a -2 penalty on saving throws against death effects for 1 minute.

**Grasped:** When grasped, an undead under the wielder's control may consume the implement's affliction from a target as part of making an attack. If they do, the affliction's penalty to saving throws becomes a penalty to the creature's AC against the undead's attack, and the undead gains a circumstance bonus to damage equal to this implement's enhancement bonus.

**Construction**
**Requirements** Craft Implement Of Power, Death sphere (any (dominion) talent); **Cost** +3,000 gp

#### Cloud-Shifting [DRS]

*Source: Diamond Spheres: Magical Organizations*

**Aura** moderate Nature; **CL** 8th
**Price** 5,000 gp

**Description**
This implement seems to whistle and sing in the wind, small tufts of cloud-like smoke trailing from it.

**Prerequisite Sphere:** Nature sphere.

As a standard action, the wielder may create a brief current of wind within 60 feet. This wind is not enough to disperse a fog effect, but instead can move a fog effect up to 5 feet, +5 feet per enhancement bonus. For unusually large fog effects (generally those greater than a 20-foot radius), a *cloud-shifting* implement may move up to a 20-foot radius of the fog effect with a single use.

The brief current of wind produced by a *cloud-shifting* implement may move any fog or fog-like effect that would normally be dispersed by wind; if a fog effect would be produced by a constant source (such as an *eversmoking bottle*, the *guards and wards* spell, or a similar constant source of fog), the fog effect dissipates 1d4 rounds after being moved from its source.

**Grasped:** While grasped, the wielder may concentrate on the current of wind, instead creating wind that can disperse a fog effect. This wind disperses fog within 5 rounds, -1 per enhancement bonus (at enhancement bonus 5, using the *cloud-shifting* implement disperses the fog the same turn it is conjured).

**Construction**
**Requirements** Craft Implement Of Power, Nature sphere ((air) package); **Cost** +2,500 gp

#### Energy-Branding [DRS]

*Source: Diamond Spheres: Magical Organizations*

**Aura** moderate Destruction; **CL** 7th
**Price** varies (see table)

**Description**
This implement features a faintly glowing iron brand, which appears faintly warm to the touch.

**Prerequisite Sphere:** Destruction sphere.

Each *energy-branding* implement has an associated damage type, selected when first crafted. This determines the cost and its effects (see Table: Energy-Branding Implement below).

As a standard action, the wielder of an *energy-branding* implement may brand a creature within 60 feet with glowing red runes which last for 1 minute. If the marked creature suffers the associated damage while branded, the brand is consumed and the creature takes additional damage of the associated type equal to 1d6 + this implement's enhancement bonus and suffers a -2 penalty on attack rolls for 1d4 rounds.

**Grasped:** When grasped, the wielder may place enhanced brands on a creature, which are not consumed when the branded target takes the associated damage (but only triggers to deal damage once per round). Alternatively, the caster may increase the casting time of a sphere ability by 1 step to have that sphere ability brand any target that is damaged or fails their saving throw (as though targeted with the non-grasped *energy-branding* effect).

**Construction**
**Requirements** Craft Implement Of Power, Destruction sphere (any fire (blast type)); **Cost** varies (see table)

#### Fate-Turning [DRS]

*Source: Diamond Spheres: Magical Organizations*

**Aura** strong Fate; **CL** 13th
**Price** +13,000 gp

**Description**
This implement feels like it possesses a notable heft to it, despite any weight it may have, as though it knows it is important.

**Prerequisite Sphere:** Fate sphere.

As a standard action, the wielder of a *fate-turning* implement may target another creature within 30 feet, imposing a beneficial or detrimental effect of the wielder's choice.

- *Beneficial:* The target receives a +2 bonus on attack rolls and saving throws against Fate sphere effects.
- *Detrimental:* The target receives a -2 penalty on attack rolls and saving throws against Fate sphere effects.

This effect lasts for 1 round per enhancement bonus. Only a single creature at a time may be affected by the *fate-turning* implement; using this implement again ends the effect on any previous target.

**Grasped:** The wielder may concentrate on this implement's effects as a standard action (the same as concentrating on a sphere effect). While concentrating, the implement's effects do the following, in addition to their beneficial or detrimental effect:

- *Beneficial:* The target adds a +1d4 insight bonus to any d20 roll they rerolled (such as the Fate sphere Bless talent). This bonus applies to the check's final result.
- *Detrimental:* The target subtracts -1d4 from any d20 roll they rerolled (such as the Fate sphere Curse talent). This penalty applies to the check's final result.

**Construction**
**Requirements** Craft Implement Of Power, Fate sphere (Bless or Curse); **Cost** +6,500 gp

#### Light-Shining [DRS]

*Source: Diamond Spheres: Magical Organizations*

**Aura** faint Light; **CL** 4th
**Price** +6,000 gp

**Description**
This implement casts a soft, welcoming light in your hands, and an imposing glare when held aloft.

**Prerequisite Sphere:** Light sphere.

As a standard action, the wielder of a *light-shining* implement may make the implement cast bright light in a 20-foot radius as a torch. This light may be snuffed by the wielder as a move action (or part of a move action to move). Allies within this light gain a +2 circumstance bonus on Perception checks.

**Grasped:** When grasped, the light produced by a *light-shining* implement greatly interferes with the wielder's enemies. Whenever an enemy makes an attack roll while within a *light-shining* implement's radius of bright light, they must succeed at their choice of either a Reflex saving throw (DC equal to the wielder's Light sphere DC) or a Perception check (DC equal to 10 + 5 times this implement's enhancement bonus). A failed saving throw or skill check blinds the attacker until the end of turn (this may cause the triggering attack to roll against concealment). Regardless whether the creature succeeds or fails their saving throw or Perception check, that creature is immune to this effect for 24 hours.

A creature with the light sensitivity or light blindness abilities rolls their saving throw or Perception check twice and takes the worse result and only becomes immune for 24 hours if they succeed their saving throw or Perception check.

**Construction**
**Requirements** Craft Implement Of Power, Light sphere; **Cost** +3,000 gp

#### Rune-Buffering [DRS]

*Source: Diamond Spheres: Magical Organizations*

**Aura** faint Protection; **CL** 4th
**Price** +6,000 gp

**Description**
This implement features a band of repeating runes and symbols etched into the implement with metal filigree.

**Prerequisite Sphere:** Protection sphere.

As a standard action, the wielder of a *rune-buffering* implement may grant themselves or an ally within 30 feet a +2 circumstance bonus on saving throws for 1 round.

**Grasped:** When grasped, the *rune-buffering* implement's effects last for a number of additional rounds equal to this implement's enhancement bonus. Alternatively, this ability can be used as a swift action but lasts the normal duration of 1 round.

**Construction**
**Requirements** Craft Implement Of Power, Protection sphere; **Cost** +3,000 gp

#### Platform-Calling [DRS]

*Source: Diamond Spheres: Magical Organizations*

**Aura** moderate Creation; **CL** 8th
**Price** +8,000 gp

**Description**
This implement features a geometric, crystalline pattern.

**Prerequisite Sphere:** Creation sphere.

As a standard action, the wielder may create a single horizontal, flat 5-foot-by-5-foot platform made of crystallized magic anywhere within 25 feet + 5 feet per enhancement bonus. Each platform is a physical object with 5 hit points, 2 hardness per enhancement bonus, and can support 200 lbs per enhancement bonus (or generally 1 Medium creature per enhancement bonus). Platforms created with this implement float and can remain suspended over other surfaces or even midair.

Any platforms created using the *platform-calling* implement last for up to 1 minute or until the *platform-calling* implement is used again.

**Grasped:** While grasped, the wielder may create a single platform as a swift action, rather than as a standard action.

If used as a full-round action, the wielder may instead create a number of contiguous platforms, up to 1 platform + 1 platform per enhancement bonus (such as to create a 2x2 platform using 4 platforms, or a 1x6 platform to span a gap). Contiguous platforms add their weight total together when determining the total weight they can hold.

**Construction**
**Requirements** Craft Implement Of Power, Creation sphere (Object of Force); **Cost** +4,000 gp

#### Shadow-Veiling [DRS]

*Source: Diamond Spheres: Magical Organizations*

**Aura** moderate Illusion; **CL** 8th
**Price** +9,000 gp

**Description**
This implement seems to drink in light, making it more difficult to discern.

**Prerequisite Sphere:** Illusion sphere.

As a standard action, the wielder of a *shadow-veiling* implement may grant a veiling haze to themselves or an ally within 30 feet. This haze grants 20% concealment for 2 rounds per enhancement bonus. Concealment granted by a *shadow-veiling* implement does not allow a creature to make a Stealth check to hide.

While benefitting from this haze, as an immediate action the affected creature may increase this concealment to 50% (but the haze's effects end at the end of the current turn). This immediate action must be taken before an attack roll is made.

**Grasped:** When grasped, the *shadow-veiling* implement's ability can be used as a swift action. Alternatively, when used as a standard action, the wielder may grant a veiling haze to any number of allies within 30 feet.

**Construction**
**Requirements** Craft Implement Of Power, Illusion sphere; **Cost** +4,500 gp

#### Spatial-Stitching [DRS]

*Source: Diamond Spheres: Magical Organizations*

**Aura** moderate Warp; **CL** 8th
**Price** +7,000 gp

**Description**
This implement is made of concentric, overlapping parts that seem to unnaturally overlap.

**Prerequisite Sphere:** Warp sphere.

As a standard action, the wielder may designate a 5-foot cube within 25 feet + 5 feet per enhancement bonus, creating a "spatial stitch". A spatial stitch remains until the wielder creates a new spatial stitch or the spatial stitch becomes further than 25 feet + 5 feet per enhancement bonus from the *spatial-stitching* implement's wielder.

Whenever a creature moves through a spatial stitch, that creature may move through that square without expending movement (the spatial stitch is effectively a square of free movement, ignoring difficult terrain and not provoking for movement through the square, but not allowing a creature to move through solid objects or spaces they could not pass through normally). Movement through a spatial stitch is a teleportation effect.

**Grasped:** While grasped, the wielder may create a spatial stitch as part of a move action made to move. In addition, the wielder may create 1 additional spatial stitch so long as the implement is grasped (and may place the two spatial stitches down simultaneously, provided they are adjacent to one another).

**Construction**
**Requirements** Craft Implement Of Power, Warp (Loop Space); **Cost** +3,500 gp
