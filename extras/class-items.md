# Class Items [DRS]

*Source: Diamond Spheres: Magical Organizations*

This section contains magic items which complement specific classes or class features.

These items cannot be used by a character that lacks the associated class feature (or feature selection), even through Use Magic Device. If an item would be available to multiple classes (such as the *bloodline curio* for sorcerer and bloodrager), a character may have a single such item for each appropriate class feature provided the benefit is for that specific class feature.

If one of these items would grant a creature or ability that offers further choices, those choices are made when first benefitting from the item (and can never be changed).

### Alchemist's Chemkit
**Aura** faint transmutation (Creation) — **CL** 4th
**Slot** slotless; **Price** 10,000 gp; **Weight** -

**Description**
This compact chemistry kit is stocked for conducting a specific experiment.

Each *alchemist's chemkit* is attuned to a single alchemist discovery (chosen when crafted). When worn or used by a creature with the alchemist discovery class feature, the wearer gains the attuned alchemist discovery. The wearer must still meet the discovery's prerequisites (if any).

A character may only benefit from a single *alchemist's chemkit*; to equip an *alchemist's chemkit*, a character must attune to the *alchemist's chemkit* by concentrating on it for 1 minute.

**Construction**
**Requirements** Craft Wondrous Item (Craft ApparatusUSoP), *bomber's eye* or Creation (Alchemical Creation), creator must possess the desired discovery; **Cost** 5,000 gp

### Bloodline Bauble
**Aura** faint transmutation (Alteration) — **CL** 4th
**Slot** slotless; **Price** 10,000 gp; **Weight** -

**Description**
Encased behind thick glass, the interior of this bauble is a thick, crimson fluid with the faintest tinge of the otherworldly, magical, or fantastic.

Each *bloodline bauble* is attuned to a single sorcerer bloodline (chosen when crafted). When worn or used by a creature with the sorcerer's bloodline class feature, the wearer gains the attuned sorcerer bloodline's 1st level bloodline power (using their effective sorcerer level with their bloodline class feature to determine the granted bloodline power's effects).

If the wearer already possesses the attuned bloodline power, the wearer gains that many additional uses per day instead (for whatever the normal number of uses of that ability is); this may also allow the wearer to gain a bloodline power they exchanged for a bloodline mutation, bloodline familiar, or other ability. The bloodline power granted by the *bloodline bauble* cannot be exchanged for a bloodline mutation, bloodline familiar, or other ability.

A character may only benefit from a single *bloodline bauble*; to equip a *bloodline bauble*, a character must attune to a *bloodline bauble* by concentrating on it for 1 minute.

**Note - Bloodragers:** The sorcerer and bloodrager "bloodline" feature are identically named but use vastly different game mechanics. A *bloodline bauble* may be attuned to a bloodrager bloodline, functioning the same except only for a creature with the bloodrager's bloodline class feature.

**Construction**
**Requirements** Craft Wondrous Item (Craft ApparatusUSoP), *alter self* or Alteration sphere, creator must possess the desired bloodline; **Cost** 5,000 gp

### Clergy Icon
**Aura** faint divination (Divination) — **CL** 4th
**Slot** slotless; **Price** 13,000 gp; **Weight** -

**Description**
This small painting depicts one facet of a deity's legend and lore in a small paneled casing. Light filigree and symbology for the deity decorate the frame.

Each *clergy icon* is affiliated with a single deity, and a single domain belonging to that deity.

*Clergy's Miracle* - The user may expend 1, 2, or 3 uses of channel energy to replicate the 1st, 2nd, or 3rd level spell associated with the inscribed domain (a number of channel energy uses equal to the spell level replicated). The caster level of a spell replicated using a *clergy icon* is equal to the number of channel energy dice the user possesses (or twice the channel energy dice, if the user is a worshipper of the *clergy icon*'s associated deity).

When determining the DC of the replicated spell, the DC is equal to 10 + the spell's level + the user's ability score associated with their channel energy ability.

**Construction**
**Requirements** Craft Wondrous Item (Craft Marvelous ItemUSoP), *augury* or Divination sphere (Augury), creator must possess the desired domain; **Cost** 6,500 gp

### Curse-Bearing Vessel
**Aura** faint enchantment (Protection) — **CL** 4th
**Slot** slotless; **Price** 6,000 gp; **Weight** -

**Description**
This ivory-white effigy is carved with an ornate pattern.

*Curse Sealing* - Once per day, a *curse-bearing vessel* may be concentrated on as a standard action that provokes attacks of opportunity by a user with an oracle's curse. For 10 minutes, the penalties of the user's oracle curse are suppressed. While suppressing a curse, the *curse-bearing vessel* shifts to a dark, inky black.

The user may instead use the *curse-bearing vessel* as a swift action, but their curse's penalties are suppressed for only 4 rounds.

**Construction**
**Requirements** Craft Wondrous Item (Craft ApparatusUSoP), *oracle's burden* or Protection sphere (Fateless); **Cost** 3,000 gp
