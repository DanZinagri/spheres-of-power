#### Lock In [DRS]

*Source: [Diamond Spheres: Expanded Tinker and Silverminds](https://www.drivethrurpg.com/en/product/556860/diamond-spheres-expanded-tinker-and-silverminds)*

You gain the following personal battery use ability.

- **Personal Battery Use [Lock In] (0-2+ batteries, see text):** You may deplete 1 battery to regain martial focus as a move action, or 2 batteries to regain martial focus as a swift action. This personal battery use costs 1 fewer battery for every battery you have depleted in the same round, to a minimum of 0 batteries.

**Special - 10 ranks in the Associated Skill:** If you have depleted 2 or more batteries in the same round, you may instead deplete 1 battery to regain martial focus as an immediate action or free action during your turn.

**Special - (Computation) Package:** AI you crafted may also use the lock in personal battery ability, and gain a bonus task known to do so.
