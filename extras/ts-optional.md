### Feat Prerequisites with Consistent Access [DRS]

*Source: [Diamond Spheres: Expanded Tinker and Silverminds](https://www.drivethrurpg.com/en/product/556860/diamond-spheres-expanded-tinker-and-silverminds)*

**Rule:** Characters may select any feat as though they met its prerequisites so long as they can do so consistently or frequently for extended periods, but only benefit from that feat (and any feats with it as a prerequisite) when they meet the prerequisites.

**Context and Reasoning:** Characters can often meet feat prerequisites in certain circumstances or only when combined with class features or talents.

At GM discretion, feats that have a continual effect or requirement, such as Transformation feats or Obedience feats, must have their prerequisites met normally. Additionally, feats that grant or alter permanent subordinates should have their prerequisites met normally.

**Example:** A kobold with the Tinker sphere that has built and frequently pilots a strong mechanoid, but is itself weak, could qualify for the Power Attack feat. It can only benefit from the Power Attack when piloting the mechanoid or otherwise increasing its strength to at least 13.

### Delayed Tinker Tradition Bonus Talents [DRS]

*Source: [Diamond Spheres: Expanded Tinker and Silverminds](https://www.drivethrurpg.com/en/product/556860/diamond-spheres-expanded-tinker-and-silverminds)*

**Rule:** Tinker Tradition bonus talents are not all gained immediately. Instead, the practitioner gains a single Tinker bonus talent and then whenever the practitioner spends a non-tradition bonus talent on the Tinker sphere, they gain an additional bonus Tinker talent until they have gained all bonus talents granted by their Tinker tradition (generally 1 talent per 2 Tinker drawbacks).

Talents directly granted by a Tinker tradition are gained normally, such as the bonus talent granted by the Central Intelligence boon.

**Context and Reasoning:** Complex tinker traditions can lead to an abnormally generous number of talents up front for a single talent, or where Tinker tradition drawbacks may be circumvented or easily ignored. This optional rule changes it where the bonus talents normally granted by your Tinker tradition are instead furloughed and granted on investing additional resources into the Tinker sphere until the character has “caught up”.

**Example:** Tony is an innovator technician. He has a Tinker tradition with 6 drawbacks and the Central Intelligence boon, which means he’ll get 4 bonus talents from his Tinker tradition in total (three from the complexity of his Tinker tradition, plus the bonus talent from the Central Intelligence boon).

When Tony first gains the Tinker sphere, Tony would gain a single bonus Tinker talent plus the bonus talent from Central Intelligence, and would need to spend two additional, non-bonus talents on the Tinker sphere to gain the 2nd and 3rd bonus talents Tony is “owed” by their Tinker tradition’s complexity.
