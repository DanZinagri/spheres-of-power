#### Mode Switching (requires Tinker sphere) [DRS]

*Source: [Diamond Spheres: Expanded Tinker and Silverminds](https://www.drivethrurpg.com/en/product/556860/diamond-spheres-expanded-tinker-and-silverminds)*

The armiger gains the Tinker sphere Multifunctional Gizmos talent as a bonus talent. Whenever the armiger switches from one talent set (or other equivalent feature, at the GM’s discretion) to another, he may also switch a gizmo or *mechanoid’s* upgrade that has multiple selections granted by one of Multifunctional Gizmos’ options (Multifunctional Augmentation, Multifunctional Modification, Transforming Parts, etc.). The armiger may only switch selections of gizmos in their possession or the possession of a mechanoid they are piloting. In addition, the armiger gains the following personal battery use ability:

- **Personal Battery Use [Mass Set Switch] (1 battery):** This battery use is used as part of switching a gizmo or upgrade with multiple selections. When you do, you may switch up to 2 gizmos, +1 per 4 armiger levels.
