#### Empowered Familiar [DRS]
*Source: Diamond Spheres: Magical Organizations*

**Prerequisite:** Familiar.

The witch gains the arcane bond amplification wizard arcane discovery. The witch cannot apply a "free" familiar archetype to their familiar if that archetype could not ordinarily be selected by a witch's familiar (such as the figment familiar archetype being inaccessible to witches).
