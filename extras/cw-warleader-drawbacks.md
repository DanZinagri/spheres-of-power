*Source: Diamond Polished Spheres: Commander and Warleader Sphere*

#### Loudmouth

**Prerequisite:** (shout) package.

Your *shouts* do not discriminate between ally and enemy, affecting all creatures equally (or treating that creature as whatever is most favorable to them). You may choose to be unaffected by your own harmful *shouts*. You gain a bonus Warleader talent that grants a (shout) option.

You gain the following option, which may be applied as part using any harmful (shout) that offers a saving throw:

##### Early Warning

**Descriptor:** language-dependent

**Action:** +1 step

**Effect:** Creatures that can understand you gain a +4 circumstance bonus on their saving throw against the *shout*.

**Incompatible:** Hype Beast.

#### Focused Command

When you first gain this sphere, you choose either the (shout) or (tactic) package (instead of gaining both). You gain an additional (shout) or (tactic) talent based on which package you selected.

#### Hype Beast

Your Warleader sphere effects only affect other allies and cannot affect you or enemies. You gain Battlecry Mastery as a bonus talent.

**Incompatible:** Loudmouth.

#### Music Man

You do not select an associated skill when you gain this sphere. You gain Bandmaster as a bonus talent and must use the chosen Perform skill and its instruments when you use your *shouts* or *tactics*.

#### Simple Strategies

You cannot expend martial focus to use or augment your Warleader sphere abilities. You gain both Warleader sphere packages when you first gain this sphere.
