### Aspect Talisman [DRS]

**Source:** Diamond Spheres: Magical Organizations

**Aura** moderate transmutation (Alteration) — **CL** 8th
**Slot** none; **Price** 8,000 gp; **Weight** —

**Description**
This nondescript talisman has faint inscriptions of a person, although difficult to make out even under scrutiny. When held for a length of time, the inscriptions slowly change to match its wearer, a cartoonish mockery of the holder.

The wearer gains Aspect of the Beast as a bonus feat. Whenever the wearer rests for 8 hours, the wearer may reselect the manifestation granted by this feat. The wearer must be in possession of this talisman for 24 hours for its magic to attune to them, and if separated or lost, must be attuned for 24 hours.

**Imbued Manifestation** - If the wearer possesses the Alteration sphere, when selecting a manifestation from Aspect of the Beast, the wearer may instead select a single Alteration sphere trait they know, gaining its effects with an effective caster level of 1 (paying any spell point costs as required). This is a constant supernatural polymorph effect and stacks with other polymorph effects.

**Construction**
**Requirements** Craft Wondrous Item (Craft Apparatus [USOP]), *alter self* or Alteration sphere; **Cost** 4,000 gp

### Creature Caller's Phial [DRS]

**Source:** Diamond Spheres: Magical Organizations

**Aura** moderate conjuration (Conjuration) — **CL** 9th
**Slot** slotless; **Price** 9,000 gp (standard), 22,000 (greater); **Weight** —

**Description**
This translucent, silvered phial has engraved images of various monsters that match the phial's imbued potential. When opened, the liquid inside rapidly turns to a gaseous substance and infuses with the called creatures.

Each *creature caller's phial* has a single imbued Conjuration (type) talent.

When summoning one or more creatures, the user may open the *creature caller's phial* to mix the imbued (type) talent with the summoned creatures. Each creature summoned this way gains the imbued (type) talent (although only a single instance of the talent, even if they already possess this (type) talent). This may allow a Conjuration companion to possess more than one (type) talent.

Only a single summoning spell may be affected by the *creature caller's phial*. When the summoning effect ends, the contents rapidly reform within the vessel and may be used again.

A *greater creature caller's phial* instead grants the chosen (type) talent as though taken twice.

**Construction**
**Requirements** Craft Wondrous Item (Craft Marvelous Item [USOP]), *evolution surge* or Conjuration sphere (any (type) talent); **Cost** 4,500 gp (standard), 11,000 (greater)

### Clockwinder Gloves [DRS]

**Source:** Diamond Spheres: Magical Organizations

**Aura** moderate transmutation (Time) — **CL** 6th
**Slot** hands; **Price** 6,000 gp; **Weight** —

**Description**
The nondescript leather gloves have small metal tines that run from the palm to the end of the fingers; each tine is shaped like the hand of a clock with a different pattern or style to it.

Twice per day as a swift action, the wearer of a pair of *clockwinder gloves* may use either of the following:

- **Quickening Snap:** The wearer gains the benefits of *haste* for 1d4+1 rounds.
- **Slowing Run:** The wearer makes a melee touch attack, affecting the target with *slow* for 1d4+1 rounds (Will save DC 13 negates). Alternatively, the wearer may run the fingers of these gloves along a weapon, imbuing its next successful attack with the *slow* effect (as though it were a *spell-storing* weapon).

**Temporal Touch** - Whenever the wearer casts a Time sphere effect targeting one or more creatures (or similar time-themed magic), the wearer may use the *clockwinder gloves* as a swift action to affect a single target of the sphere ability with either *quickening snap* or *slowing run* (this counts against the gloves' normal uses per day). When used this way, *slowing run* uses the wearer's Time sphere saving throw DC - 4 (instead of the item's normal saving throw DC, if greater).

**Construction**
**Requirements** Craft Wondrous Item (Craft Marvelous Item [USOP]), *haste* and *slow* or Time sphere (Improved Haste or Improved Slow); **Cost** 3,000 gp

### Cloying Brooch [DRS]

**Source:** Diamond Spheres: Magical Organizations

**Aura** faint evocation (Dark) — **CL** 5th
**Slot** none; **Price** 6,000 gp; **Weight** —

**Description**
This pale, circular brooch appears to be made of brushed silver, with no descriptive adornments or patterns. When viewed closely in dim light or darker (DC 15 Perception), the thin stain of a shadowed hand appears to stretch across the center of the brooch.

**Stretch Shadow** - Three times per day, the wearer may manipulate their shadow as a swift action, shaping it into a shadow limb for 5 minutes. The shadow limb can manipulate and hold objects, including performing delicate tasks such as Disable Device and Sleight of Hand skill checks, but otherwise cannot be used to make attacks, wield weapons or shields, or fulfill somatic components. The shadow limb's natural reach is twice the wearer's normal reach (or a minimum of 5 feet, if they would have a reach of 0). For example, a size Medium creature would be able to manipulate objects up to 10 feet away and a size Large could reach 20 feet.

The shadow limb is partially-immaterial and allows the wearer to use the shadow limb to squeeze through and into small gaps (i.e. the bars of a jail cell, beneath a door or a gap in a desk drawer, etc.). This does not allow the shadow limb to transport an item through a gap it could not pass through, but may allow the wearer to manipulate objects they could not ordinarily reach (such as unlocking a door, manipulating objects on the other side of a window, and so on).

**Subtle Shadow** - While the stretch shadow ability is active, the user may end its duration as part of using the subtle action skill use (*Spheres of Guile*) to gain a +5 circumstance bonus to their subtle action check.

**Construction**
**Requirements** Craft Wondrous Item (Craft Marvelous Item [USOP]), *darkness* or Dark (Manifest Darkness) (*Diamond Polished Spheres: Dark Sphere*); **Cost** 3,000 gp

### Coin of Weighted Fortune [DRS]

**Source:** Diamond Spheres: Magical Organizations

**Aura** moderate divination (Fate) — **CL** 8th
**Slot** slotless; **Price** 9,000 gp; **Weight** —

**Description**
This featureless and ridgeless gold coin feels incredibly smooth to the touch.

The user may toss this coin as a swift action, rolling 1d2 (or flipping a coin):

- **Heads (I Win):** The user gains a +2 insight bonus on their next attack roll, saving throw, or skill check made before the end of the user's next turn.
- **Tails (You Lose):** The user designates another creature within 30 feet. That creature suffers a -1 penalty on their next attack roll, saving throw, or skill check made before the user's next turn. This is a curse effect.

This coin may only be flipped during moments of stress, action, or adrenaline. As such, it may only be used during combat (or other high stress scenarios, subject to GM discretion). The coin may be flipped any number of times during combat.

**Lucky Word** - Once per day, the user may flip this coin as part of using the Fate *word* sphere ability. The heads or tails result affects the target (or targets) of the *word* (this may affect an ally with tails, or opponent with heads - that's the nature of a coinflip).

**Construction**
**Requirements** Craft Wondrous Item (Craft Marvelous Item [USOP]), *borrow fortune* or Fate sphere (Bless or Curse); **Cost** 4,500 gp

### Gravewhisperer Veil [DRS]

**Source:** Diamond Spheres: Magical Organizations

**Aura** moderate necromancy (Death) — **CL** 9th
**Slot** head; **Price** 9,000 gp; **Weight** —

**Description**
This gray silken veil features a pair of embroidered skeletal hands which seem to hold the veil shut.

When the wearer or an undead under their control kill another creature within 100 feet, a fragment of their spirit is temporarily trapped within the *gravewhisperer veil*. A fragment can be stored for up to 24 hours this way, and once released, returns to the original creature's soul. The wearer may choose to release a fragment as a free action or as part of using the expel fragment ability below.

Only a single fragment can be stored this way, and while stored, the veil's gray color lightens to a bone white and infrequently flickers, as though appearing and disappearing as a ghost may. **Expel Fragment** - Whenever the wearer casts a necromancy school spell or Death sphere *ghost strike*, you may release a fragment trapped within this veil. When released, the spell is modified with either the Ectoplasmic Spell or Enlarge Spell metamagic feat without increasing the spell or sphere effect's spell level or spell points used.

**Construction**
**Requirements** Craft Wondrous Item (Craft Marvelous Item [USOP]), *speak with dead* or Death sphere (Gravetongue); **Cost** 4,500 gp

### Sanguinis Coil [DRS]

**Source:** Diamond Spheres: Magical Organizations

**Aura** strong necromancy (Blood) — **CL** 13th
**Slot** wrist; **Price** 19,000 gp; **Weight** —

**Description**
This pale coil wraps tightly around the wrist, set with soft prongs against the underside of the wrists. The coil appears translucent.

Whenever the wearer suffers bleed damage, the *sanguinis coil* absorbs a portion of it as a blood charge, to a maximum of 3 blood charges. Alternatively, the wearer may make a melee touch attack dealing 1d6 piercing damage, and, if the target is able to bleed, the target suffers 1 bleed damage and the *sanguinis coil* collects 1 blood charge.

**Blood Purge** - As a move or swift action, the wearer may deplete 3 blood charges from the *sanguinis coil* to inject the stored vitae directly into them. Upon doing so, the wearer makes a Fortitude saving throw against an ongoing, non-permanent effect that offered an initial saving throw (this saving throw is made against the effect's original saving throw DC and is a Fortitude saving throw instead of the effect's original saving throw type). The *sanguinis coil* can only allow the wearer a single new saving throw this way, and failing this new save counts as an additional failure for effects such as poisons.

If the wearer possesses the Blood sphere, the wearer may spend 2 spell points instead of 3 blood charges to use blood purge.

The wearer may use this ability so long as they are capable of taking mental actions (such as when paralyzed, but not if dazed or stunned).

**Construction**
**Requirements** Craft Wondrous Item (Craft Marvelous Item [USOP]), *touch of bloodletting* or Blood sphere (Manipulate Health); **Cost** 9,500 gp
