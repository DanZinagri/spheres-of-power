This is a list of resources to help with character or campaign crafting.

- [[Character Builder]] - Build a Pathfinder 1e character, with or without Spheres, and export it as a file you can import into Foundry VTT.
- [[Item Crafter]] - Price a Spheres of Power magic item and work out its crafting cost, time, check DC and requirements.
- [[Monster Creator]] - Load a Pathfinder monster, apply templates, add Hit Dice, class levels or sphere talents, and export a Foundry NPC or a stat block, with the CR worked out for you.
