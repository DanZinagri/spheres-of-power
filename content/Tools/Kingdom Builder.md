Build and run a kingdom with the kingdom-building rules from Pathfinder RPG Ultimate Campaign ([on Archives of Nethys](https://www.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=1489)). Set the kingdom's alignment, Size, Treasury and Unrest, choose its Holiday, Promotion and Taxation edicts, count its Farms, Mines, Roads and other terrain improvements, and fill the leadership roles with each leader's ability modifier. Then add settlements and the buildings in them.

The builder totals Economy, Loyalty and Stability and shows where every point comes from, with the Control DC, Consumption, and each check's chance of success after Unrest. For each settlement it gives the lots used, population and size, base value, Defense, settlement modifiers, magic item slots and the buildings' cost, and flags buildings over their limit or missing the House they must stand beside. The turn summary runs the Upkeep and Income phases on the d20 you enter: the Stability check, Consumption, Unrest from vacant roles, and the taxes an Economy check collects. <a href="../static/kingdom-builder/index.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Open the builder in its own tab</a> if you want more room.
