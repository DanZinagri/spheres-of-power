Roll up a treasure pile. Set what it's worth, either by challenge rating (the Core Rulebook's treasure per encounter, with slow, medium or fast advancement, incidental to triple treasure, and any number of encounters' worth) or as a gold range, then pick a theme. An armory is mostly arms and armor, a treasury mostly coin, gems and jewelry, a tomb grave goods with the odd cursed piece, a wizard's study scrolls and wands, a dragon hoard a bed of coin under gems and prized arms, a bandit camp stolen purses and the gang's own gear, and so on: the theme sets how the budget is split and which tables are rolled on.

The pile is built to the budget: magic items first, then mundane gear, art and jewelry and gems, and whatever is left in coin. Magic items come from Pathfinder (the Core Rulebook's random tables and Archives of Nethys items, with potions, scrolls and wands made from spells) and from Spheres (this wiki's [[Loot Tables]] and its priced items), mixed by default. Tick **Spheres of Power game** to swap in the Spheres versions where there is an obvious one: compounds for potions, spell engines for wands, and the Spheres charms for ability, resistance, deflection and natural armor items. Copy the result as text when you're happy with it. <a href="../static/loot-generator/index.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Open the generator in its own tab</a> if you want more room.
