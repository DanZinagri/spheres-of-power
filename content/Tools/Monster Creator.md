Start from any Pathfinder monster (from Archives of Nethys). It's rebuilt from its parts: racial Hit Dice, ability scores, natural armor, feats and skills. A check shows where the book's numbers differ from what its parts add up to. Then change it: apply templates, add racial Hit Dice or grow it a size, add class levels, give it sphere talents or a martial, casting or trade tradition, edit its attacks, gear and defenses.

The challenge rating follows the Bestiary's monster advancement rules: added Hit Dice by the hit points they add (Table 2-1), class levels by whether the class is key to the monster's role, and each template's own CR change. Might and Guile talents count like bonus feats; Power talents count for more, like adding spells. Export the monster as a Foundry NPC (Import Data) or as a text stat block. Your monster is saved in this browser as you go. <a href="../static/character-builder/monster.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Open the creator in its own tab</a> if you want more room.
