Build a Pathfinder 1e character here, then click **Export for Foundry** to get a file for the Foundry VTT Pathfinder 1e system. In Foundry, create a character, right-click it in the Actors sidebar, choose **Import Data** and pick the file. Your work is saved in this browser as you go. <a href="../static/character-builder/index.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Open the builder in its own tab</a> if you want more room.
