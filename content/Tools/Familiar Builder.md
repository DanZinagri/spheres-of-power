Pick a familiar from the wizard's list or the Improved Familiar list (from Archives of Nethys). It starts from the creature's own stat block, then follows the familiar rules: its master's base attack bonus, the better of its own and its master's base saves and skill ranks, half its master's hit points, and the natural armor, Intelligence and special abilities for the master's level. The master's numbers can be typed in or pulled from the character saved in the [[Character Builder]] on the same browser.

Tick **Pet (Beastmastery sphere)** for the [[Beastmastery]] sphere's Pet talent: the level comes from the master's base attack bonus or Handle Animal ranks, the creature stays an animal with its own Intelligence, and it doesn't get deliver touch spells, scry on familiar, share spells, speak with animals of its kind or spell resistance. The Improved box covers the Improved Familiar feat and the Improved Pet talent. Feats, gear, sphere talents and a portrait work as they do in the other builders. Export a Foundry actor (Import Data), a PDF sheet or a text stat block. <a href="../static/character-builder/familiar.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Open the builder in its own tab</a> if you want more room.
