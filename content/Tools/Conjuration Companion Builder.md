Build a companion for the [[Conjuration]] sphere. Choose its base form (avian, biped, ooze, orb, quadruped, serpentine or vermin) and set its caster's caster level, typed in or pulled from the character saved in the [[Character Builder]] on the same browser. Hit Dice, base attack bonus, saves, skill points, feats, natural armor and the special abilities it gains follow Table: Companion, and the ability score increase every 4 Hit Dice is yours to assign. It can be Small instead of Medium.

Then shape it. Tick the (form) and (type) talents it has, including the advanced ones: each is added to the sheet with its text, and you apply what it changes to the companion's size, speeds, senses and attacks. Tick its companion archetypes too, including the avatar archetype the [[Voidrusher]] and void conduit grant. The archetypes that change the basic numbers are worked in for you: familiar (half caster level), mage, mindless, unwilling, beast, warrior and avatar. Feats, skills, gear, sphere talents and a portrait work as they do in the other builders. Export a Foundry actor (Import Data), a PDF sheet or a text stat block. <a href="../static/character-builder/conjuration.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Open the builder in its own tab</a> if you want more room.
