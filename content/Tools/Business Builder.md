Plan a business with the downtime rules from Pathfinder RPG Ultimate Campaign ([on Archives of Nethys](https://www.aonprd.com/Rules.aspx?ID=1245)). A building is a set of rooms and an organization is a set of teams: start from one of the book's example buildings or organizations, or add rooms and teams one at a time, and change how many of each you have.

The builder totals the Goods, Influence, Labor and Magic it takes to create, what that costs bought outright or earned with your own downtime, what is still to pay after the capital you already hold, and how long the work takes (rushed if you like, for extra Labor or Influence). It then works out the daily capital check for gp and for each kind of capital, with every room and team that can earn it, taking 10 or on the roll you enter, with or without you there running the place, and takes a manager's wage off the gp. <a href="../static/business-builder/index.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Open the builder in its own tab</a> if you want more room.
