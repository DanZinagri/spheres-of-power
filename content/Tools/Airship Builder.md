Design an airship with the [[Airship Rules]]. Set the hull's size in Hardpoints or Decks, add engines and a dirigible, divide the hull into rooms and mount siege engines, or start from one of the sample airships and change it.

The builder works out the ship's size and Locations with their AC and hit points, its CMB and CMD, ram damage, mass, weight, power and propulsion, speed, acceleration and maneuverability, its load against the hull's limits, the crew its engines need, and an itemized price. It warns when the ship can't lift its own weight, when the rooms don't fit the hull, and when sails, dirigible or cargo are over their limits. Copy the result as a stat block when you're done. <a href="../static/airship-builder/index.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Open the builder in its own tab</a> if you want more room.
