This is a list of resources to help with character or campaign crafting.

<div class="sop-tools">

- <span class="sop-icon sop-icon-tool-character" aria-hidden="true"></span>[[Character Builder]] - Build a Pathfinder 1e character, with or without Spheres, and export it as a file you can import into Foundry VTT.
- <span class="sop-icon sop-icon-tool-crafter" aria-hidden="true"></span>[[Item Crafter]] - Price a Spheres of Power magic item and work out its crafting cost, time, check DC and requirements.
- <span class="sop-icon sop-icon-tool-loot" aria-hidden="true"></span>[[Loot Generator]] - Generate a treasure pile to a budget and a theme: coins, gems, art and jewelry, mundane gear and magic items, from Pathfinder and Spheres tables.
- <span class="sop-icon sop-icon-tool-business" aria-hidden="true"></span>[[Business Builder]] - Put a building or organization together from the Pathfinder downtime rules' rooms and teams, and see what it costs, how long it takes and what it earns a day.
- <span class="sop-icon sop-icon-tool-kingdom" aria-hidden="true"></span>[[Kingdom Builder]] - Keep a kingdom's sheet by the Pathfinder kingdom-building rules: leaders, edicts, hexes, settlements and buildings, with its Economy, Loyalty, Stability, Control DC, Consumption and what a turn brings in.
- <span class="sop-icon sop-icon-tool-airship" aria-hidden="true"></span>[[Ship Builder]] - Design a land, water or air vehicle: hull and material, engines, armor, rooms, siege engines and equipment, with its speed, defenses, load, crew, price and monthly upkeep by the Skybourne vehicle rules.
- <span class="sop-icon sop-icon-tool-monster" aria-hidden="true"></span>[[Monster Creator]] - Load a Pathfinder monster, apply templates, add Hit Dice, class levels or sphere talents, and export a Foundry NPC or a stat block, with the CR worked out for you.
- <span class="sop-icon sop-icon-tool-companion" aria-hidden="true"></span>[[Animal Companion Builder]] - Build an animal companion at any level, with the Beastmastery sphere's options, and export a Foundry actor, a PDF sheet or a stat block.
- <span class="sop-icon sop-icon-tool-familiar" aria-hidden="true"></span>[[Familiar Builder]] - Build a familiar, an improved familiar or a Beastmastery pet from its master's statistics, and export a Foundry actor, a PDF sheet or a stat block.
- <span class="sop-icon sop-icon-tool-conjuration" aria-hidden="true"></span>[[Conjuration Companion Builder]] - Build a Conjuration sphere companion from its base form, caster level, form talents and companion archetypes, and export a Foundry actor, a PDF sheet or a stat block.
- <span class="sop-icon sop-icon-tool-cohort" aria-hidden="true"></span>[[Cohort Builder]] - Build a Leadership sphere cohort from its leader's associated skill ranks and its job, and export a Foundry actor or a PDF sheet.

</div>
