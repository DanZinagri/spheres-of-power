Design a vehicle with the Skybourne vehicle rules: the wiki's [[Airship Rules]] from The Player's Guide to Skybourne, filled out with the options of Ships of Skybourne. Airships are the usual case, but the same rules build wagons, steam walkers, galleys and submersibles.

Set the hull's size in Hardpoints or Decks and what it is made of, choose whether it travels by land, water or air (or more than one), and add templates, armor, engines or creatures to pull it, a dirigible, rooms, siege engines and other equipment. Or start from a sample and change it. The builder works out the vehicle's size and Locations with their AC and hit points, its CMB and CMD, ram damage, speed in each medium, acceleration and maneuverability, its load against the hull's limits, and an itemized price.

Under **Crew and upkeep**, set the crew's level and how many shifts you run, add officers by role and level, and choose rations: the builder counts the crew the engines and siege engines need and totals the monthly wages, food and fuel. It warns when the vehicle can't lift its own weight, when the rooms don't fit the hull, and when something is over its limit. Copy the result as a stat block when you're done. <a href="../static/ship-builder/index.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Open the builder in its own tab</a> if you want more room.
