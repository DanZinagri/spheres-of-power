Build a cohort for the [[Leadership]] sphere's cohort package. A cohort has no class levels: enter its leader's associated skill ranks (Diplomacy or Profession), or pull them from the character saved in the [[Character Builder]] on the same browser, and Table: Cohort sets its Hit Dice, base attack bonus, saves, feats and talents.

Then choose its job. The job sets the starting ability scores, good and bad saves, class skills (plus two of your choice) and the benefits it gains at job levels 1, 2, 3 and 5, which are added to the sheet as it reaches them. Stat growth every 4 ranks and the ability score increase every 4 Hit Dice are yours to assign. Caster jobs can be classic casters, with their spells per day shown, or spherecasters. Race, skills, feats, sphere talents, a martial tradition, gear and a portrait work as they do in the Character Builder. Export a Foundry actor (Import Data) or a PDF sheet. <a href="../static/character-builder/cohort.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Open the builder in its own tab</a> if you want more room.
