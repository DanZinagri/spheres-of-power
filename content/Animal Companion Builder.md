Pick a companion from the druid's animal companion list (from Archives of Nethys), then set the level it comes from: a class's effective druid level, or the [[Beastmastery]] sphere's Animal Companion talent, which uses the master's base attack bonus, Handle Animal ranks or Ride ranks. The master's numbers can be typed in or pulled from the character saved in the [[Character Builder]] on the same browser.

The companion's Hit Dice, base attack bonus, saves, skill ranks, feats, natural armor, Strength and Dexterity bonus, bonus tricks and special abilities follow the animal companion table. Its 4th- or 7th-level advancement applies when the level reaches it (or take +2 Dexterity and Constitution instead), and the ability score increases at levels 4, 9, 14 and 20 are yours to assign. A checkbox turns on the Beastmastery variant rule that gives companions a base attack bonus equal to their Hit Dice. Feats, skills, gear, sphere talents and a portrait work as they do in the other builders. Export a Foundry actor (Import Data), a PDF sheet or a text stat block. <a href="./static/character-builder/companion.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Open the builder in its own tab</a> if you want more room.
